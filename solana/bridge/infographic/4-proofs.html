Who proves what — the two directions
A proof is something the receiving chain checks by itself. A trusted party is someone it believes. Teal boxes are checks; orange boxes are trust; grey ones carry or store and decide nothing.
gno → Solana · from the first block · the proof is gno.land's own block signatures
1 · you
lock 100 GNOT in the lock realm on gno.land; hlmailbox records the message
2 · gno.land's validators
sign block H with > 2/3 of the voting power. Those signatures are the proof
3 · the updater
a node of the operator's — anyone may run one — sends block H's header and signatures to Solana
4 · the relayer
the operator's, anyone may — sends the message with a merkle path into block H
5 · Solana checks
the pausable gate: no flag, no quarantine. The light client: did > 2/3 of the power really sign H (ed25519)? Is the message really in H?
6 · mint
100 synthetic GNOT to your Solana wallet
No prover on this path. Nobody is trusted: Solana's program checks gno.land's validators' signatures itself. Only those validators could fool it, with > 2/3 of the power — and a change of the set pauses minting for 7 days.
Solana → gno · after Alpenglow (Proposal 2) · the proof is a ZK proof of a finalized Solana block
1 · you
burn 100 synthetic GNOT; the mailbox writes a burn record into block N: which program sent it, how much, to whom
2 · Solana's validators
finalize block N: an Alpenglow certificate, the BLS signatures of ≥ 60% (or 80%) of the stake over the block id
3 · THE PROVER
a node that keeps every account's old and new state for each block — the operator's GPU box, or anyone's such node, or a hired prover network — runs the ZK program and emits a Groth16 proof (10–90 min, $1–6)
4 · the relayer
sends the proof and the burn record to gno.land
5 · gno.land checks
the verifier realm checks the proof in one native call (≈1.8M gas). Inside it: the certificate ✓, block id → bank hash ✓, every account change in N adds up ✓, the burn record among them ✓
6 · credit
a claim for 100 GNOT, paid out of the lock at the meter's rate
The prover is not trusted: a wrong proof fails step 5. A missing prover only delays — then GovDAO credits the burn by vote. The one thing still vouched for: who Solana's validators are each epoch (the roster), by the 3 signers, until SIMD-0511 puts it on chain.
Solana → gno · until Alpenglow (at launch) · no proof exists yet: 3 machines vouch
1 · you
burn 100 synthetic GNOT on Solana
2 · the 3 signers
the operator's machines, each reading Solana through 2 RPC providers; 2 of 3 sign that the burn happened
3 · the relayer
sends the burn message and the 2 signatures to gno.land
4 · hlmailbox's verifier
are these 2 of the 3 signers' signatures? (trust in the machines, not a proof)
5 · credit
a claim, paid at the meter's rate; the governor (GovDAO from the yield) voids a forged one; a guardian can pause payouts
6 · you
receive GNOT on gno.land, in turns with other claims
This is the only place machines are trusted, and the meters bound what a lie can take: one day's cap at most before anyone looks (1/10 of the lock, or 10,000 GNOT if that is more), and the governor (GovDAO from the yield) voids the rest.
what "proof" means here
A block's fingerprint, not a tree
Solana keeps one running total of all its accounts (the lattice hash). You cannot open a total and point at one account, so no single account can be proven. But: fingerprint after block N − fingerprint before = the sum of every changed account's (new − old).
The lattice-delta proof
List every account block N changed, old and new, and show the differences add up to the fingerprint change. The burn record is one of them. The list runs to thousands of accounts, so a ZK program does the adding off chain and gno.land checks only its stamp.
The honest limit: griefing
Anyone can bloat a block's change list (dust to huge accounts): ≈4–9 SOL a day to do it to every block, under a cent for one block by a block producer. A griefed block's burn is credited by GovDAO vote instead — so "trustless" holds for un-griefed blocks.
