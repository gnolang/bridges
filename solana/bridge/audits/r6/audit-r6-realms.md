## Findings

### R35 — medium — a successor enrolled on Solana and then re-pointed away is still every burn's recipient: it is no route, takes its own messages as a launch route against a lock of 0, nobody can flag it, the line's last realm cannot take them, and a stranger strands any of them for good
Rule: PLAN §5.3 ("a predecessor whose successor has pulled nothing accepts a second migrate that re-points it, so no lock is ever stranded on a realm nobody may pull from"; "hlmailbox lets the successor take a message addressed to any route in its chain of predecessors"); §5.4 ("a credited amount above the lock goes to the sweep queue"); §13 (kind 6 "for gno.land's own domain the builder and the closure accept only a registered route's address").
Files: realm/hlmailbox/flags.gno:324–343 (`AddRoute` re-points: `dropRoute(old)`), 348–359 (`dropRoute` forgets the record and its flags — nothing remembers the dropped path), 446–458 (`successorOf` walks `predecessor` links only, so a dropped successor is in nobody's chain), 253–255 (`SetFlag` refuses a path that is no route); realm/hlmailbox/mailbox.gno:456 (the recipient, or a successor of it); realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1897–1905 (`MustTake(true, "")` passes: a realm the mailbox no longer registers reads as a launch route), 513–531 (`Credit`: `amount > s.Lock` with a lock of 0 sweeps); realm/hlgnot/hlgnot.gno:233–239 and 266, realm/hlbubble/hlbubble.gno:217–223 and 250 (`Process` asks `hlmailbox.Predecessor(pkgPath)`, "" for a dropped realm); realm/hlgov/hlgov.gno:542–552 and 557–569 (a kind 6 for the successor is legal the day the migrate applies, and nothing undoes it on Solana but another kind 6).
Scenario: day 7 hlgnot's migrate to S1 applies; GovDAO proposes kind 6 for S1 that day, as §5.3 urges ("a week at least"), and it executes on Solana on day 14: from then every GNOT burn's message names S1. S1's deployer goes quiet and S1 pulls nothing — the very case AR2's re-point cures — so GovDAO proposes the re-point to S2 on day 14; it applies on day 21 and S1 is dropped. Kind 6 for S2 can be proposed only now (S2 was no route before) and executes on day 28 at the earliest, later under a hold: two weeks of burns name S1. The relayer delivers each to the line's last realm, S2, and the mailbox refuses ("only the message's recipient, or a successor of it": S1 is in nobody's chain), so they park and alarm. Anyone — the sender, with the published signatures and the published tool — calls `S1.Process`: S1's `MustTake(true, "")` passes, the mailbox marks the id done, `Credit` sweeps it against S1's lock of 0: owed at a realm that holds no coins and is no route. No guardian can flag S1 (`SetFlag` refuses a non-route), S1's sweep payment and credit-by-id refuse (a sum above a lock of 0), and S2's `NewCreditRequest` for the id refuses ("done already"): gone for good. The ones nobody strands are GovDAO's chore — a hand credit at S2, one id per 7-day proposal — AR3's wedge again, one hop later.
Fix: (1) the mailbox remembers a dropped successor on its predecessor's record (`routeRec.former []string`, appended where `AddRoute` calls `dropRoute`) and `successorOf` matches a hop's former successors' addresses as it matches the hop, so the line's last realm takes a message addressed to a dropped successor once its last page is in; (2) a route realm refuses a message when the mailbox no longer registers it — `MustTake` takes `registered bool` (the realm passes `hlmailbox.IsRoute(pkgPath)`) and panics "a route no more (re-pointed to another successor): the line's last realm takes this message" before the mailbox is asked. Test: a message addressed to the dropped successor — refused at the dropped realm, waiting at the new successor before its last page, taken by it after; the mailbox's `TestRepointSuccessor` gains the dropped path as recipient.

### R36 — medium-low — kind 6's builder enrols any registered route for either token (the JUDGE item): a wrong enrolment strands one token's burns behind per-id votes, delays its sends two weeks, and writes the AR1 enrolment note on the other token's route, re-opening the double mint the note was made to close
Rule: PLAN §5.3 ("kind 6's builder, for gno.land's own domain, accepts only a registered route's address"; "r/hlgov's kind-6 closure records in hlmailbox the route it enrolled and the height, and a refund is refused until that record is 7 days old"); §13 kind 6; AUDIT.md round 5 ("Left as found, named for round 6").
Files: realm/hlgov/hlgov.gno:542–552 (`check`: `routePathOf(k) != ""` — any registered route, the other token's or a migrated one), 557–569 (the closure notes `NoteEnrolled(cross(cur), path, h)` on whatever route the key names), 573–580 (`routePathOf` walks every registered route, migrated predecessors included); realm/hlmailbox/flags.gno:429–441 (`NoteEnrolled` checks the caller and the path, never the token); realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1233–1241 (`mustBeEnrolled` reads the note of this route, whatever token's kind 6 wrote it); realm/hlgnot/hlgnot.gno:242–244 (`RouterAccepts`: a GNOT burn delivered to a BUBBLE route is refused — the sender is the GNOT program).
Scenario: GovDAO passes kind 6 for token 0 (GNOT) naming hlbubble2, the BUBBLE route's successor (a slip in a proposal that names the token, the domain and the router by hand; `hlwatch` prints the parameters, not the route's token). On Solana the GNOT program's router for gno.land becomes hlbubble2. (a) Every GNOT burn now names hlbubble2 as recipient: hlbubble2 refuses it (`RouterAccepts` fails — the sender is the GNOT program) and hlgnot cannot take it (not the recipient, not in hlbubble2's chain). A corrective kind 6 naming hlgnot lands ≥ 14 days on (7 to propose and deliver, 7 on Solana's clock); the burns of those weeks keep hlbubble2 as recipient for ever and are creditable only by hlgnot's hand credit, one id per 7-day proposal. (b) Every GNOT send from gno.land is refused on Solana meanwhile (`ours`: the sender is hlgnot, not the enrolled router); retried hourly, they land after the correction — delayed two weeks, not lost. (c) The closure writes `NoteEnrolled(hlbubble2, h)`: 7 days later hlbubble2's refund gate opens for the BUBBLE sends it imported while the BUBBLE program's router is still hlbubble — the refund lands, a stranger's `Reemit` and delivery of a refunded send mints BUBBLE twice, and the identity breaks by that amount for good (closable only by a write-off: AR1's own scenario, re-opened by the note meant to close it). (d) A kind 6 naming a migrated predecessor passes too (its record stays registered): the successor's sends are refused on Solana until corrected. What the vote and `hlwatch` stand in front of is a one-byte slip whose cost is weeks and a double mint.
Fix: hlmailbox answers a route's token — `RouteSymbol(path) string`: `SymbolGNOT`/`SymbolBUBBLE` for the launch paths, `SuccessorFor(path)` for a registered successor (every successor announced its route, AQ5), "" otherwise — and kind 6's `check` requires `RouteSymbol(path) == parseToken(token)` and `hlmailbox.Successor(path) == ""` (never a migrated route), at creation and in the closure; the note then lands only on the token's own live route. Test: kind 6 token 0 naming hlbubble, or a BUBBLE successor, refused at creation and in the closure ("hlbubble2 is the BUBBLE route's, not GNOT's"); one naming a migrated hlgnot refused; the note absent after each refusal.

### R37 — low — the refund's 7-day wait is counted from the kind 6's dispatch, while Solana executes a kind 6 7 days after it is recorded there: the realm's week always ends before the enrolment can take effect
Rule: PLAN §5.3 ("a refund is refused until that record is 7 days old — before it the predecessor's relayer may still deliver, and a refund landing first lets a stranger's `Reemit` mint the send twice"); §13 ("Kinds 3–9 and 12 are the 7-day group"); §6.5 ("7 days from recording for the 7-day group"; a hold defers it, §6.1).
Files: realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1233–1241 (`mustBeEnrolled`: `now < at+decisions.Delay`, `at` the dispatch's block time); realm/hlmailbox/flags.gno:429–441 (`NoteEnrolled` stamps `now()` at dispatch); realm/hlgov/hlgov.gno:557–569 (the note is written in the dispatching closure, before any delivery).
Scenario: kind 6 for S is dispatched at T and recorded on Solana a day later (the relayer's hourly retry, a parked staging, a flag on the pausable ISM — §5.7 stages no route message while a flag stands, and decisions ride the same float); the admin program executes it at T + 8 days. S's refund of the imported sends is proposed at T, executes at T + 1, is applied by anyone at T + 8 days minus a minute: the predecessor is still the router for a day, and the refunded sends are deliverable in it — AR1's window, opened by the realm's own clock. Under a hold (§6.1) the deferral is weeks. M1h calls the gate "necessary, not sufficient" and leaves the rest to hlwatch's alarm; the plan's sentence reads as if the week sufficed, and it never can by construction.
Fix: `mustBeEnrolled` waits `2*decisions.Delay` (a week for the delivery, a week for Solana's clock; "a refund is irreversible and a week is cheap" — so is a second), the texts and §5.3 saying "14 days after the kind 6's dispatch, since Solana executes it 7 days after recording"; hlwatch's alarm on an unenrolled successor stays the sufficient check, and §5.3 says a hold defers it further. Test: a refund at 13 days refused, at 14 taken.

### R38 — low — a write-off has no inverse: a mistaken one books `lost` for the deployment's life, and the identity then hides a forgery up to its amount
Rule: PLAN §5.3 ("restore a voided claim (the full inverse of either void)"; "a `write off` builder, 7 days, books a send delivered after its refund as lost, so the identity closes"); §5.9 ("the watch flags only when the right side exceeds the lock (the reverse, a mistaken void, alarms)").
Files: realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1249–1264 (`DraftWriteOff`: no delivered check — the realm cannot make one), 1787–1794 (`writeOff`: `Lost += amount`, the mark), 1125–1141 and 1655–1678 (`DraftRestore` and its apply take claims and sweep entries only — nothing moves `Lost` back for a send), 2250–2269 (`send/<id>` shows `writtenOff`; nothing clears it).
Scenario: GovDAO writes off a refunded send on a DELIVERED line that was wrong (a record the two providers showed differently, §5.9's UNCONFIRMED, read as delivered; or the neighbouring id in a list of twenty). `Lost` rises by the amount for good: the identity reads lock > supply + owed + voided − lost by that much, the watch's "mistaken void" alarm stands for the life of the deployment, and — since the flag fires only when the right side exceeds the lock — a later forged credit up to that amount moves the alarm's figure and trips nothing. Every other booking of the identity has its inverse (restore for both voids and for a voided sweep entry); this one has none.
Fix: `DraftRestore` accepts a written-off send's id (`sd.WrittenOff != 0`: `WrittenOff = 0`, `Lost -= amount`, the counters down), after 7 days like a forged claim's restore, since it re-opens the identity; the note and `send/<id>` say so. Test: a restore of a written-off send refused at once, applied after 7 days, the identity closing on the old supply.

### R39 — low — a burn naming a successor route's address is credited as a claim and paid into a realm's account: `isRealm` names the six launch realms and no successor
Rule: PLAN §5.2 ("a recipient equal to any of the six realms' addresses … is refused at send on gno.land; a Solana burn to such a recipient is credited to the sweep queue"); §5.4 (a swept credit is "credited by id to its owner" by GovDAO).
Files: realm/hlgnot/hlgnot.gno:71–74 (`realms`, compiled: the six), 105–112 (`isRealm`), 266 (`Credit(…, sweep: err != nil || isRealm(to))`); realm/hlbubble/hlbubble.gno:63–66, 96–103, 250; realm/hlmailbox/flags.gno:121–131 (`IsRoute`, `Routes`: the mailbox knows every registered route, and the list is short).
Scenario: hlgnot migrated to S a year ago; a holder burns 1,000 GNOT on Solana to S's address — the "address" S's json and page print as the holder of the lock, pasted as a deposit address. S credits a claim to its own address and `Claim` pays its own account 1,000 GNOT out of the lock: the coins sit in S's account outside every counter (`Balance` ≠ `Locked`), with no builder to move them and no path for the holder; the same burn to hlgnot's address is swept and credited to its owner by id. The successor template is this realm's code, so every successor inherits the gap.
Fix: `isRealm` also matches every registered route's address — `for _, p := range hlmailbox.Routes() { if chain.PackageAddress(p) == a { return true } }`, a handful of reads — in both routes, at the send's refusal and the credit's sweep. Test: a burn to a successor's address swept, then credited to its owner by id.

## Checked and held

- Spec §5.1–§5.2, guide §5.6/§5.8, review 1 and 9: every function M1h added or changed derives identity from its own live `cur.Previous()` — `NoteEnrolled` by `mustBeHlgov` (the exact pkgpath), `AddRoute`'s re-point by `mustBeRoute`, `Pull` by the exact pkgpath against `MigratedTo`; the `Env` closures `Predecessor`, `IsRoute` and `Enrolled` are the route's own non-crossing reads of the mailbox, built per call; no secondary `rlm` parameter in hl* or p/ (the checker's one hit is realm/bubbletreasury). hlgnot's `unsafe.OriginSend()` beside `cur` is the only `OriginSend` the pinned v1.2.0 offers (`gnovm/stdlibs/chain/runtime/unsafe/unsafe.gno:64`); `PreviousRealm()` appears nowhere.
- The GovDAO boundary (spec §2, §5.1; guide §2.1): the pinned proxy's `SimpleExecutor.Execute` (examples/gno.land/r/gov/dao/types.gno:216–233) checks `cur.IsCurrent()`, requires its own caller to be the proxy's path, and calls `e.callback(cross(cur))` — so inside every builder's closure `cur.Previous().PkgPath()` is `gno.land/r/gov/dao` exactly, which `mustBeDAO` compares whole (`decisions.DAOPath`); the closure, declared in the realm, runs under the realm's authority (borrow rule #1) and crosses on from its own frame (`NoteEnrolled(cross(cur), …)`, `Dispatch(cross(cur), …)`, `ClearFlags(cross(cur), …)`, `MarkDone(cross(cur), …)`). A closure returns the deadline error alone and panics on everything else (§5.3).
- Spec §5.3, guide §5.7, review 6: the note, the succession and R35's former-successor list are ints and strings; a `Draft` carries parameters only; `env(cur)` is captured by transient closures in the executing frame (the ADR's allowed case), never persisted; the closure the DAO persists captures `d` and `made`.
- Spec §5.5: pkgpath keys stay exact — `hlgovPath`, `mustBeRoute`, `Pull`, `successorOf`; `AddRoute` and `DraftMigrate` refuse `#`; `AnnounceSuccessor` and `RegisterVerifier` refuse a sub-identity by `Subpath()`.
- Spec §3.2/§4.1–§4.3, guide §2.1–§2.3, §3(C): no caller-supplied function or interface value runs under a realm's authority but the registered `Verifier` (declared in the registering realm, GovDAO's read; X2's key copy); `copyFlags` and `dropRoute` act on the mailbox's own allocations; a page's records are plain values the successor allocates as its own.
- Spec §9, guide §6.1: every gate M1h added panics before the mailbox marks or anything moves (`mustAllowMigrate`, `mustMigrate`, `mustBeEnrolled`, `MustTake`, `AddRoute`'s refusal reverting `MigratedTo`), so a refused decision stays pending, never spent.
- Spec §11.3, guide §5.1/§5.1a, review 3/8: `Enrolled`, `Predecessor`, `IsRoute`, `SendJSON`, `Former` return ints and strings; `Pull` returns a fresh `*Snapshot` of plain values; `st`, `routes`, `flags`, `successors`, `former` unexported; no `/p/`-type pointer leaves a realm.
- Arithmetic: `Credit`'s overflow guard on `Owed`; a send at most the maximum, `SentTotal` with 2^63 of headroom; `Meter.refill`'s bounded terms; the refund moves `SentTotal` and `Lock` down with the coins, the write-off moves `Lost` up with nothing, page 0 carries every counter; every path that lowers the lock is bounded by it, so `Cap()` never sees a negative lock.

- Flags and seats: a re-point drops the dropped path's flags and copies the predecessor's standing ones; page 0 waits on the predecessor's own payouts flag; `VacateSeat`, `ClearFlags`, `MarkDone`, `AddRoute` only by a registered route (`TestRepointSuccessor`).

- `Process` and `Dispatch` against §13: unchanged by M1h (the event's attributes and order, `Reemit`'s equality, `done.Set` before `Verify`, the recipient-or-successor rule, mailbox.gno:444–484).

## Verdict

Rounds 1–5's fixes hold, M1h's six included; the realms meet the Interrealm Specification v2 and the security guide on every function M1h added or changed, and the GovDAO boundary every builder rests on is verified against the pinned proxy. Nothing critical or high. One medium: the AR2 re-point and the plan's advice to enrol a successor early combine into a realm that is every burn's recipient, no route, flaggable by nobody and in nobody's chain — two weeks of a token's burns behind per-id votes, any of them strandable for good by a stranger (R35; two small changes). One medium-low, the JUDGE item: kind 6 enrols any registered route for either token — a token's burns stranded, its sends delayed, AR1's note on the wrong route and the double mint re-opened (R36; a `RouteSymbol` and two comparisons). Three lows: R37, R38, R39. By the loop's written rule — nothing above medium — the realms converge this round; R35 and R36 belong in the fold before the launch vote.
