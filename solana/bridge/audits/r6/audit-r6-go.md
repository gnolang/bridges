### G46 — medium — a route's line that cannot be read for one tick makes the relayer mark the successor's sends "other" for good: K34's loss by a transient door
agent/relayer.go:96-109 (`readLines`: a `migrationFor` error is logged — "the configured realm alone is ours this step" — and the symbol left out of `r.lines`), 114-131 (`lineSender`: with no line, only the configured realm's address is ours); relayer_sol.go:149-151 (`classify` answers `kindOther` for a sender that is not `lineSender`), 265-271 (the walk writes `Done[nk] = "other"` and advances `GnoNext` past it), relayer.go:350-361 (`pruneDone` forgets it below `GnoNext`), relayer_sol.go:711-713 (`doneForReemit("other")`: the Reemit rule never looks again); migrationFor's reads: relayer.go:517-563 (`routeState` → `routeAt` at the head on the own node; `mailboxState`; `migrationOf` reads every realm of the line, migrate.go:190-192, 218-220, 269-271).
Scenario: hlgnot migrated to hlgnot2 and kind 6 enrolled hlgnot2 as the router; the configs still name hlgnot, as AQ1 allows. A send through hlgnot2 sits at `GnoNext`. In that tick `routeAt(hlgnot2)` fails — a `vm/qrender` timeout on the own node, a node restart between the message read and the line read, or (G44's case) a successor whose `Render("json")` errors on one field, which makes it every tick — while `gnoMessage` for the nonce succeeds. `readLines` logs and goes on; `classify` finds the sender hlgnot2 outside `{hlgnot}` and answers `kindOther`; the walk stores "other", advances, and the state is saved. Nothing revisits the nonce: the Done entry is pruned, the Reemit rule skips "other", the monitors count the send in flight and alarm `age`/`stall` only, and the send is locked on gno.land with nothing minted and no refund (a live successor's own send is not an imported one, so the refund builder cannot name it). Fix: `readLines` keeps the symbols whose line failed (`r.lineErrs`); `classify` answers a fourth kind — "line unread", held like `kindOtherAdmin` (`advance = false`, a note, no Done entry) — for a route message whose sender is not the configured realm while that route's line is unread; `checkRouters` skips such a route for the tick. A test: `readLines` with the successor's render failing, a send from the successor at `GnoNext`, the state unchanged after the tick.

## Checked and held

- The line walk: `followLine` refuses a loop and the seventeenth hop, keeps the hops in order, and `predecessorsOf` walks the mailbox's entries back; `migrationOf` finds a configured middle or last realm's line; `Others` lists the live realm first; `Sum` adds every realm; `Ended` is the last successor's import alone; `WaitText` names the state. `realms()` and `liveRoutes()` include every realm of the line; `claimOnPair`/`sendOnPair` try the live realm first.
- The relayer's per-step cache: `resetStep` empties the routes, the mailbox and the lines; `deliverUnderLightClient` reads the lines after the mailbox; `reemits` likewise; `claims` and `executes` reset first. A dispatch whose line cannot be read is kept pending with a note; a claims or executes step with the error skips the token for the tick.

## Verdict

Round 5's fixes hold at their lines and each new test proves its fix rather than merely running it — with one test enshrining the failure state G46 names. Three mediums, all at the seams of M3i's own fixes: a route line that cannot be read for one tick makes the relayer walk past the successor's sends as "other" for good (G46, K34's loss by a transient door); hlwatch and the relayer list only the configured realms' proposals and queued decisions, so AR1's pre-vote refund check, the migrate check and the proposal finisher never see a successor the config does not name — the standard layout after a migration (G48); and a checkpointed index calls every open claim credited before the checkpoint a forged credit, flagging credits, payouts and the pausable ISM on a fresh install's second poll (G49, G36's fix firing falsely). Two medium-lows: the monitor's checkpoint mismatch is a webhook line, never a stroke, and the trusted header is compared once (G47); a failed or unconfirmed stroke is printed as landed (G50). Three lows. Nothing above medium; fold G46, G48 and G49 before devnet, and give the e2e one migration, one refund proposal and one checkpoint, since every round-5 fix lives behind a seam the e2e has never crossed.
