## Findings (the AS series)

### AS1 — medium — a re-pointed migration drops the first successor from the line while Solana still names it the router: its sends mint against a lock no tool counts, and burns addressed to it credit a realm outside the line

Plan: §5.3 "a predecessor whose successor has pulled nothing accepts a second migrate that re-points it, so no lock is ever stranded on a realm nobody may pull from"; "the relayer delivering to the line's last realm and taking the line's sends as its own"; "burns made during the migration are addressed to the predecessor until kind 6 enrolls the successor (a week at least)"; AUDIT.md round 5 "the relayer … checking the enrolled router". Nothing orders the re-point against the kind 6 that already enrolled the dropped successor.

Code: `AddRoute` re-points by dropping the first successor's registration and its flags (flags.gno:332–342, `dropRoute` 348–359); `Dispatch` is open to any realm (mailbox.gno:384–402) and `TransferRemote` asks only `mustBeLive`, the route's own flags and `CheckSend` — never whether the realm is still a route (hlgnot.gno:172–213); after the drop `hlmailbox.Predecessor(S)` is "" (flags.gno:383–389), so S's `Process` takes a message addressed to itself as a launch route would (`MustTake(true, "")`, hlroute.gno:1897–1905; hlgnot.gno:233–240) and sweeps it against a lock of 0 (`Credit`, 513–534); `SetFlag` refuses a path that is no route (flags.gno:253–255), so no guardian can pause S; the tools' line is `migratedTo` from the configured path (migrate.go:172–200), so `lineSender` (relayer.go:114–130), `identityOf`, the signers' `signerIdentities` (signer.go:254) and hlwatch sum hlgnot and S2 and never S; `checkRouters` only alarms `router:unknown` (relayer_sol.go:157–190).

Scenario: AR2's own. Day 0 GovDAO migrates hlgnot → S and, as §5.3 advises, proposes kind 6 enrolling S together; the migrate applies day 7, kind 6 lands day 14 — from then the token program mints only for S's dispatches and every burn names S. S's deployer goes quiet (page 0 never pulled). Day 8 GovDAO proposes the re-point hlgnot → S2; day 15 it applies: S is dropped, kind 6 for S2 is proposed that day and lands day 22 at the earliest. That week S is still Solana's router. (a) Any holder calls `S.TransferRemote` (10 GNOT plus the fee): S locks the coins and dispatches under its own address; the relayer calls it "other", but the sender delivers it himself with the published tool — the pausable ISM meters it, the token program accepts its enrolled router S and mints. Solana's supply exceeds the line's lock by 10 GNOT for the deployment's life: BROKEN on every monitor and both hlwatches, the signers stop (§5.6), every exit is by vote — §1's leak ending for 15 GNOT, and no builder books it (a write-off takes a refunded send, a void a claim). (b) A burn addressed to S that week is nobody's to the relayer (`deliveryTarget` nil, relayer.go:585–598); a stranger delivers it to S with the signers' public signatures and S sweeps it into a queue no vote can pay (S holds no coin) — S2's hand credit refuses the id as "done already" (hlroute.gno:1164–1166): the holder's coins are lost. A re-point back to S is the only cure, and S is the silent one.

Fix: hlmailbox keeps a dropped successor as a tombstone — `dropRoute` records the path under its predecessor's `dropped` list — and three gates read it: `Dispatch` refuses a caller that is a dropped route (its sends can mint for nothing else), a dropped route's `Process` is refused by `MustTake` through a new `Env.Dropped` (or `Predecessor` answering the tombstone), and `successorOf` lets the line's live successor take a message addressed to a dropped predecessor-sibling, so the burns of the week wait for S2 as they wait for a predecessor's. §5.3 says a re-point is proposed with the kind 6 that re-enrols, and that between them the dropped realm neither sends nor credits and burns addressed to it wait for the new successor. The tools' line should also alarm, not merely note, while the enrolled router is a dropped route (`router:unknown` as a flag-level alarm on `sends`).

### AS2 — medium — the refund's gate is r/hlgov's dispatch of a kind 6, not Solana's execution of it: a cancelled, lost or held kind 6 leaves the note standing, and hlwatch reads the note, not the token

Plan: §5.3 "r/hlgov's kind-6 closure records in hlmailbox the route it enrolled and the height, and a refund is refused until that record is 7 days old — before it the predecessor's relayer may still deliver … `hlwatch` alarms a refund proposal whose successor the token has not enrolled and prints DELIVERED beside any id Solana processed". "Whose successor the token has not enrolled" is a Solana fact; §6.5 makes a kind 6 execute 7 days after *delivery*, deferred by a hold, cancelled by a kind 1, or lost to a foreign number ("accepted and ignored").

Code: `NoteEnrolled` is written in r/hlgov's closure at the proposal's execution (hlgov.gno:557–569) with the dispatch height and time (flags.gno:429–441); nothing clears it — `cancel` and `sync sequence` leave it, a later note overwrites. The realm's gate is `now ≥ at + 7 days` at the draft and the apply (hlroute.gno:1233–1241, 1721). hlwatch's `refundCheck` reads `mb.Enrolled[realm]` alone — NOT ENROLLED, UNDER THE 7-DAY DELAY, then "the realm takes the refund" (main.go:262–313); it never reads the token program's enrolled router (`RouterFor`, which `checkRouters` reads for the relayer) nor the kind 6's state, and it runs on proposals only (`programChecks`, 353–371), never on the queued refund in its 7 days.

Scenario: GovDAO proposes kind 6 (S as GNOT's router) day 7 and dispatches it; the next day hlwatch shows the kind 6 pending with the wrong token byte (the kind-6 builder checks the route is registered, not that it is the token's own — the gap round 5 named), GovDAO cancels it at once and means to re-propose. The note stands at day 7. Day 15 a member proposes the refund of the 40 imported sends; hlwatch prints "enrolled 8 days ago: the realm takes the refund" and no alarm; day 22 it applies. hlgnot is still the enrolled router. A refunded sender calls `Reemit` and delivers his own 25,000 send (≈0.03 GNOT, three Solana transactions): he holds 25,000 GNOT on gno.land and 25,000 synthetic — minted twice, as AR1 said, for every refunded id whose sender notices. The same without a cancel: a hold on the admin program that began after the refund's proposal defers the kind 6 past the refund's apply (the hold's lift leaves the day AR1 described); or the kind 6's number taken by a foreign decision. The write-off books the loss as lost; the lock is short by it for good.

Fix: hlwatch's `refundCheck` reads the token program's enrolled router for gno.land's domain and the kind 6's state on the admin program, and alarms NOT ENROLLED ON SOLANA (the router is X, not this realm; the kind 6 is pending/deferred/cancelled/absent) whatever the note says — on the proposal and on the queued refund decision every poll until it applies; r/hlgov's `cancel` builder clears the mailbox's note for a kind 6 it cancels (it knows its own numbers' kinds), so the realm's gate restarts with the re-proposal; §5.3 says the note is r/hlgov's dispatch, that a cancel clears it, and that the standing order is to cancel a queued refund (at once) while hlwatch shows the kind 6 unexecuted.

### AS3 — medium-low — a checkpointed dispatch index trusts nothing before its slot: every honest claim waiting at a fresh install is "forged" on the second poll, a false pause of both sides

Plan: §5.9 "a fresh install may start from a checkpoint of the client's record rather than the deploy — the dispatch index starting there too, the claims credited before it trusted as the headers are"; "a credited id that neither provider's dispatch index holds flags `credits` and `payouts` on the second poll that finds it so, both scans complete".

Code: `seedCursors` starts each token's scan at the newest signature at or below the checkpoint's slot (checkpoint.go:133–155; its comment repeats the plan's "trusted"), so the index holds only burns dispatched after it; `unindexedCredits` walks every waiting claim of the line (`openClaims`, chain.go:434–464, no height cut) and flags any id the index lacks once it has stood a poll (`persisted(key, m.Every)` then `breakFlags`, monitor.go:1230–1247) — nothing reads the claim's credit height against the checkpoint, and nothing reads a dispatch record by id (Hyperlane keys the account by a unique pubkey, not the id, so there is no by-id account read to fall back on). `breakFlags` sets the watch key's `credits` and `payouts` on gno.land and the flag on the pausable ISM, which only an unpause clears (judge, 642–645: "never the flags").

Scenario: month 3, the operator replaces monitor 2's machine and starts it with `-record-from client`, as §5.9 allows. Forty honest claims are waiting on hlgnot (burns of the last two days, paid in installments). The second poll finds none of their ids in an index that starts at the checkpoint: forty "forged credit" flags — gno `credits` and `payouts` by the watch key, the pausable ISM flagged — deliveries and payouts stop until GovDAO's unpause (at once on gno.land, 24 hours on Solana). A burn parked a week and delivered after the checkpoint does the same on a clean install, one at a time.

Fix: the checkpoint seeds the index from the mailbox's dispatched-message accounts — one `getProgramAccounts` on the mailbox filtered to the dispatched-message discriminator, through both providers, the accounts never closed (what §5.8's "indexing Solana's dispatched-message accounts by message id" already assumes) — so the index is complete whatever the provider's history; failing that, the rule trusts a claim credited at or below the checkpoint's gno height and alarms, never flags, one credited after it whose burn may predate the slot. §5.9 names which.

### AS4 — low — nothing live compares gno.land's per-message maximum with the ISM's: a raise in the wrong order strands every send between them for a week, alarm-free

Plan: §5.4 "a raise goes to Solana first (kind 12, 7 days) and to gno.land after: r/hlgov's kind-12 builder refuses a maximum below the route's current one, and the route's maximum builder says so in its description"; "a send between the two strands on Solana (`OverMax`, parked until a kind 12 lands)"; §5.3 "a stranded send there waits for a kind 12, since a refund voids nothing on Solana".

Code: `DraftMaximum` refuses a raise only below the minimum and writes "raise the Solana limit (kind 12) first" into the description (hlroute.gno:917–926); the realm cannot read Solana. hlwatch prints the ISM's limits beside the lock (main.go:821) and alarms when they move (1133–1134), the monitor alarms the cap ratio (monitor_sol.go:354–374); neither reads the route's `Maximum()` against `MaxPerMessage`, and `programChecks` reads no route-maximum proposal. The relayer parks the send `OverMax` "waiting for a kind 12 raise" (relayer_sol.go:405–406).

Scenario: GovDAO raises hlgnot's maximum to 50,000 (7 days) and proposes the kind 12 a week later than it meant to; for the week in between every send of 25,001–50,000 GNOT is accepted on gno.land and parked for good on Solana, unrefundable (a live route's), its holder's coins locked until the kind 12 matures. Nobody is alarmed: the park is a note the operator reads.

Fix: the monitors and hlwatch alarm `limits:mismatch:<token>` while a route's live maximum exceeds the ISM's, and hlwatch reads a route-maximum proposal against the ISM's live limits as it reads a refund proposal (RAISE ABOVE THE SOLANA MAXIMUM: sends between would strand); §5.4 names the alarm.

### AS5 — low — §5.9's acceptance list drills no migration: the cure §1 sells for a wedged route — migrate, pull, kind 6, refund, write-off, a re-point — has never run end to end

Plan: §1 "undelivered sends are refunded by migrate"; "after 7 days it can move the whole lock to a successor realm (migrate)"; §5.9's devnet runs list the hold, the lapse, the recovery drills, voids, restores, re-emits, a growing upgrade — no migrate, no `Pull`, no refund, no write-off, no kind 6 to a successor (lines 755–777); §6.4's "transfer to a successor" is the admin program's kind 9; §5.3 "No successor realm exists yet".

Code: M3i "Not exercised by the e2e (unit tests only): a migration line of two hops, a refund proposal, a double mint and its write-off, … a successor's send, … a re-pointed line"; M1h "the e2e drives no kind 6, refund, write-off or migration". Fix: §5.9 lists a migration drill among the devnet runs with a reference successor realm — a migrate under a `payouts` flag, the pages, kind 6, a burn to each realm of the line, a refund after the enrolment's week, a stranger's `Reemit` refused by the retired router, a write-off, a re-point — or says the launch rests on unit tests for that path.

### AS6 — low — §8.3 does not ask that a successor's pull be permissionless, and a re-point reaches only a successor that pulled nothing

Plan: §5.3 "its `Pull` imports a page and takes its lock in the same call, as this tree's routes do … §8.3's member reads that too"; "a predecessor whose successor has pulled nothing accepts a second migrate that re-points it, so no lock is ever stranded on a realm nobody may pull from"; §8.3 item 3 asks of a successor realm only `private` and public imports.

Code: `Pull` is the named successor's alone (hlgnot.gno:484–501); who may drive the successor's own pull is the successor's code; once page 0 moved, `mustAllowMigrate` refuses every re-point (hlroute.gno:1296–1298) and `mustNotBeImporting` every migrate of the successor (1310), so the pages after page 0 — claims, sweep entries, sends — sit on a predecessor that refuses everything, reachable by nobody's vote: a hand credit on the successor refuses their ids as "done already" (1164–1166).

Scenario: S's driver pulls page 0 from its deployer's key alone, then its deployer is gone; 1,200 claims in pages 1–3 stay in hlgnot for good. "No lock is ever stranded" is true of the lock and false of the IOUs.

Fix: §8.3's successor check adds "its pull is callable by anyone, page by page, as `Claim` is" and §5.3 says the re-point's reach ends at page 0.

### AS7 — low — the gone-decision rule's hold exception reasons from a hold that cannot have produced a validators' cancel

Plan: §5.9 "a 7-day decision gone from the admin program inside its week is foreign only while no recovery window stands and no hold overlaps its week — otherwise the validators' own cancel is the likelier cause". A recovery cancel exists only while a window stands (§6.4), and a hold opens one on its 14th day.

Code: `holdOverlaps` takes any hold that touched [delivery, delivery + 7 days] — the standing one from its start, an ended one by its span (views_sol.go:873–883); nothing asks whether it had stood 14 days inside the week or a window stood at any moment of it.

Scenario: a decision gone on day 3 by a foreign mailbox cancel; two seats flag the admin program on day 5 for an unrelated reason; the rule says "cancelled by the validators under the hold" and notes. The cost is nil today — a foreign cancel rides a forged proof header, which the header-mismatch rule flags — so this is the plan's reasoning, not its safety.

Fix: the exception reads "a window stood at some moment of the week" (fc.Window now, or a hold whose 14th day fell inside it, or a lapse); the code's `holdOverlaps` tests `h.Start + 14 days ≤ to`.

### AS8 — low — hlwatch calls an append-only successor "LAYOUT DIFFERS" when its appended field is not zero

Plan: §6.5 "a successor keeps the admin state's layout append-only and the hand-over instruction byte-identical"; M2h "any new field after `inherited` (this build's parse reads nothing past it)".

Code: `AdminStateLayout` requires every byte past the version-1 form to be zero (sol/admin.go:771–775), and `successorCheck` alarms on it as a finding (main.go:525–527); the predecessor's `can_inherit` parses a prefix (`State::deserialize`, state.rs:190–192) and would take the successor.

Scenario: a successor appends `u8 layout_version = 2` after `inherited`, as the contract allows; hlwatch tells every member before the vote that "the hand-over would be spent or die", and the vote waits on a build that zeroes a field the contract let it set.

Fix: the check reads the reserve as "bytes past the form parse as nothing this build knows" — alarm, not a finding — or M2h's contract says an appended field is zero at init; one or the other.

## Verdict

Nothing above medium. Honest for a GovDAO member on every ending §1 promises, with two qualifications about the migration, both residues of round 5's mediums: a re-pointed migration drops the first successor from every tool's line while Solana still names it the router, so until the next kind 6 its sends mint against a lock no tool counts and burns addressed to it credit a realm outside the line (AS1); and the refund's gate is r/hlgov's dispatch of a kind 6, not its execution — a cancelled, lost or held kind 6 leaves the note standing, and hlwatch reads the note where the plan says it reads the token (AS2). One false pause: a checkpointed index trusts nothing before its slot (AS3, medium-low). The rest are guards and wordings (AS4–AS8). All buildable without inventing a rule; AR1–AR7 hold at their named lines.
