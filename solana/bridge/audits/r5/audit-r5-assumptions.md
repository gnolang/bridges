The relayer proves by header only when the program's ring lacks the header (`relayer_sol.go:751`); one of a header the mirror holds records on chain, not here, and `header:gap` flags both objects after two minutes: a false pause until a hand clears it. ## Verdict

Round 4's four findings are folded at named lines in the plan, the realm and the Go (one notes residue, F25); U1–U10 stay unverifiable where they were and the plan's words about each are honest. Everything v32 and the round-4 fixes newly assume checks out against the pinned sources and the live chains — solana-go's v1 decoding and agave's version gate (a tenth of mainnet's transactions are v1 today), the record's fold and set hash, the pair's summed counters and `MustHaveImported`, the 252-validator reset, the body bound, the node fee's bound, the six sizes' rent, the features — except two things nobody ran at scale: a checkpoint over a wrapped ring evicts the wrong entries for a day and can pause both objects falsely (F21), and the monitors' per-send reads make a poll take minutes under a backlog (F23). Nothing above medium-low.
