## Findings

### G36 — medium — the dispatch index has no checkpoint: a fresh install on a provider without the full history mutes the forged-credit rule for good (G29 by the other door)
agent/chain.go:619-635 (`solScan`: a transaction that does not read is a `scanSkip`), 640-662 (`retrySkipped` keeps it with `Tries` raised, every poll, for ever), 734-741 (`solDispatchesIn` → `getTransaction` → solana-go v1.25.0 `GetTransaction` answers `ErrNotFound` for a null result, rpc/getTransaction.go:73-74 — an error, never "no dispatch"); monitor.go:1012-1014 (`len(Skipped) > 0` mutes `unindexedCredits`), 783-785 (`scan:unreadable:<SYM#i>` hourly); views_sol.go:463 (hlwatch's Forged section off), cmd/hlwatch/main.go:1226-1229 (`flag -ids` refused on an incomplete index); the checkpoint of G29 seeds the record alone (monitor.go:726-754, views_sol.go:814-837).
Scenario: a guardian installs hlwatch, or the operator a monitor after a lost state file, a year in, on a provider that keeps 90 days. `solScan("")` lists every signature of each token program since the deploy; `getTransaction` answers null for the old ones; each is a permanent skip retried every poll. From the first poll `unindexedCredits` never runs on that machine, hlwatch prints no FORGED CREDIT and refuses `flag -ids`, and `scan:unreadable` posts hourly for good naming thousands of transactions. `-record-from client` touches the record only; the cure is a hand edit of the state file's cursors.
Fix: `-record-from client` also starts each route's scan cursors at the newest signature at or below the checkpoint's slot (the claims credited before it are trusted as the headers are); a null answer is named "past the provider's retention" in the skip and retried hourly, not every poll.

### G37 — medium-low — `-record-from client` trusts the client's trusted header unexamined, and hlwatch's watch never alarms a trusted header that differs from the chain's
agent/record.go:262-274 (`StartFrom`: the counter, running hash, ring and set hash taken as the base; nothing compared with gno.land), monitor.go:729-754 (`recordCheckpoint`: no `Block(T)`), views_sol.go:814-837 (`RecordCheckpoint`, the same); views_sol.go:537-540 (`cv.Match`, computed) against cmd/hlwatch/main.go:1071-1073 (`diff` flags `Client.Mismatch` alone, never `!Client.Match`); monitor_sol.go compares the trusted header with nothing but the record; record.go:211-217, 232 (the stage seed: 5,000 slots).
Scenario: a forger under a leaked set runs the client one header ahead of gno.land (§6.1) and then feeds honest headers; the operator rebuilds a flagged monitor that day with `-record-from client`, or an outsider installs hlwatch then. The base takes the forked counter and hash, the fork sits below the checkpoint, `Consistent` says ok, and no `header:mismatch` ever rises on that machine — hlwatch prints MISMATCH beside the trusted hash in `status` only; `watch` is silent. Separately, a stage opened over 33 minutes before the checkpoint (a crashed relayer's, resumed by `driveStage`) is consumed unseen, and the fresh install flags a false `header:gap` on both objects.
Fix: both checkpoints read the own node's block at T and refuse the base when its hash differs (an alarm naming the cut as the checkpoint's slot); hlwatch's `diff` flags `header:mismatch` on `!Client.Match`; seed the stages from the client's open stage accounts (`getProgramAccounts`), not a slot window.

### G38 — medium-low — the gone-decision rule calls the validators' own recovery cancel a foreign decision, and a fresh install after a kind 9 the same
agent/views_sol.go:749-807 (`gone`: judged against `st.IsCancelled` and the walk's own mailbox `cancels` alone), programs/admin-program/src/processor.rs:436-485 (`apply_cancel`: a passed pair closes the account and stores no range when the pairs cover the range whole, S-04); monitor_sol.go:237-255 (`applyForeign` flags both objects after two minutes); cmd/hlwatch/main.go:1077-1079 (`foreign:` at flag level), views_sol.go:651 (`st.Foreign` kept for good).
Scenario: §6.4's procedure — under a hold the validators cancel GovDAO's pending replacement of the holders (`hlrecover record`, its pair passed), or in a lapse window a kind 12 of GovDAO's. Its account is gone inside its week, no range stored, no mailbox cancel names it: two minutes later both monitors flag `decision:foreign` ("a foreign decision took it; the replacement is lost") and hlwatch posts FOREIGN and keeps the line — in the middle of the recovery the plan scripts. Likewise a monitor or hlwatch without its state after a kind 9 walks the live program's numbers: every 7-day decision pending on the predecessor at the transfer (it never executes there, §6.5) is "gone" and flagged.
Fix: report a gone decision while no recovery window stands and no hold of the history overlaps its week; otherwise alarm "cancelled by the validators, or taken: read the cancel records", never flag; judge numbers at or below a retired program's `LastSeq` on that program's accounts.

### G39 — low — a decision copy addressed to a retired program whose number is past that program's next waits for ever: the walk sticks and the state grows
agent/relayer_sol.go:291-310 (the gate reads the named program's own `LastSeq`; "wait" continues with `advance` false from 265), 221-227 (`GnoNext` never passes an undone nonce), 603-618 (Retired is learned from an attempt alone), relayer.go:298-307 (`pruneDone` keeps everything at or past `GnoNext`), 853-864 (`reemits` walks from it; a retired program is `Known`).
Scenario: kind 9 executes; GovDAO dispatches L+1 and L+2 to the predecessor before the switch lands ten days later (it never waits; anyone may re-dispatch). L+1 answers Retired and is done; L+2's gate reads the retired program's `LastSeq` L and notes "waits: the admin program expects L+1 next" — for ever, since that program records nothing more. `GnoNext` stops there: the Done map and the state file grow by every later nonce and are rewritten and fsynced every tick on both relayers (G8's growth again), the status names a wait that cannot end, and the Reemit rule re-emits the dead copy at every set change.
Fix: in the decisions loop, a copy addressed to a program `adminLine().IsRetired` is ended "retired" at the gate — the same alarm and `rememberCopy` — whatever its number; Retired needs no attempt to be known.

### G40 — medium-low — hlwatch's and hlsol's one-stroke flag dies whole when the admin line cannot be read, the pausable ISM's half with it
agent/successor_sol.go:186-192 (`FlagSolana` returns before any send on a `followSuccessors` error), 43-72 (the errors: a loop, a fifth hop, a state that does not parse); cmd/hlwatch/main.go:1272-1275 (the gno flag landed, "the Solana stroke failed"), cmd/hlsol/main.go:259; against monitor_sol.go:441-446 (the monitor strokes the configured program on a nil line, the pausable ISM first).
Scenario: a leak's thief gets a forged kind 9 executed to a successor program of his (its state need only parse as ours), then writes a `successor` into that state that loops, or runs five hops. From that block every guardian's `hlwatch flag` and `hlsol flag` fails "the admin program's line of succession: … loops" and sets nothing on the pausable ISM — the flag §1's leak walk pauses the mints with — while his mints proceed under the day cap. A successor built with a later state layout the guardian's binary cannot parse does the same by accident. The monitor is not caught.
Fix: `FlagSolana` sends the pausable ISM's instruction and the configured program's when the line cannot be read, and returns the line's error beside the signature: reads fail closed, the pausable half never.

### G41 — low — a migration chain is followed one hop: a second migration without a config edit drops burns silently and flags a false break
agent/relayer.go:482-496 (`migrationFor`: one `migratedTo`), 517-536 (`deliveryTarget`: a burn addressed to neither realm is nobody's, without a note), 646-660 (claims on the first successor); migrate.go:78-91 (`migrationOf`, the same hop); monitor.go:577-582 (a break flags `mig.Live()`); nothing in config.go or the plan says the config moves to the successor.
Scenario: hlgnot migrates to hlgnot2 (the configs stay on hlgnot, as AQ1 allows) and, a year on, hlgnot2 to hlgnot3. From the second's page 0 the pair (hlgnot, hlgnot2) sums to 0 against the live supply: both monitors flag credits and payouts on hlgnot2 and the pausable ISM, re-asserted every minute; burns addressed to hlgnot3 (kind 6) are dropped by every relayer as not ours, burns still addressed to hlgnot park on hlgnot2's refusal, `Claim` is called on hlgnot2. The cure, a config edit on four machines, is written nowhere.
Fix: follow `migratedTo` transitively in `migrationFor` and `migrationOf` (four hops at most, as the admin line), summing every realm of the chain and delivering, claiming and flagging on its last; or refuse a config whose route's successor is itself migrated, naming the edit.

### G42 — low — the refund mark is read per unprocessed send every poll and per held message every tick: a backlog's cost
agent/monitor.go:893-934 (`readSends`: per unprocessed send `gnoMessage` at the head, `solDelivered` through both providers, then `sendOnPair` — one or two renders at H), 710-711 (every 10 s); relayer_sol.go:376-392 (`sendRefunded` before the cap gate, for every due route message each tick); M1g refunds imported sends alone, so a live route's mark can never be set.
Scenario: a hold pauses deliveries for its 21 days at 50 sends a day: 1,050 sends in flight, each a `send/<id>` render per poll on top of the three reads already made — ≈5,000 own-node and provider calls every 10 seconds on each monitor, repeating the last poll's answers; the relayer, a dozen sends held under the day cap, renders each every 3 seconds though none is near staging. `vm/qrender` is the VM: the poll stretches past its period and the verdict ages.
Fix: read the mark only for a route that is a successor or migrated (`IsSuccessor() || MigratedTo != ""`), once a minute per send; in the relayer, after the cap gate, for the message about to be staged.

### G43 — low — `expectedLock` is an unchecked config value: a figure above any lock the token will hold silences `mint:cap-ratio` for good
agent/config.go:83-86, 145-147 (a negative refused, nothing else); monitor_sol.go:354-362 (`capRatio`: `Reached` only at `lock >= expected`), 334-335 (`CapReached` persisted); views_sol.go:565-567; the launch notice publishes the config the guardians copy.
Scenario: the operator writes `expectedLock` in GNOT rather than base units, or a tenfold expectation to keep the launch week quiet — 10,000,000,000,000 — on both monitors and in the published bridge.json. The lock peaks at 2,000,000 GNOT and never reaches it; `Reached` stays false everywhere; an exit wave leaves the lock at a tenth of the cap, §5.4's bound off by fifty, and no `mint:cap-ratio` ever posts; `hlsol audit` prints the cap as "10.0%" of the same figure.
Fix: `capRatio` also reaches at `lock >= 5*dayCap` (the alarm is silent there anyway), so a lie delays the first judgement by at most the climb to five caps and never the standing after it.

### G44 — low — one migration partner's render error fails the monitor's whole poll

## Checked and held

- The version-1 reads: solana-go v1.25.0 decodes the 0x81 envelope and refuses address-table lookups on a v1 message (message.go:171-172), so a v1 transaction loads no addresses and the key table is its static keys; `Meta.LoadedAddresses` is appended empty, in the runtime's order for v0; `txFingerprint` compares the loaded addresses and inner instructions across the pair.
- `sol.Fits` measures the wire: `MarshalBinary` appends the missing required signatures as zeros; the delivery keeps at most nine distinct pairs, hlrecover's record more.
- The dropped pairs: the program stores the uncovered rest as a range and the executor's `executeWhen` ("cancelled: Execute closes it") sends Execute on each inside the last 500 numbers — `CloseCancelledIx` has no caller and needs none.
- `attempt.Cause` survives a transient retry; a re-marked park reserves nothing; a raise is seen at the next refusal (OverDayCap again, a cap park again).
- `ClaimsFrom`: a list that shrank below the offset reads one empty page, wraps to 0 and completes; the per-realm offset covers the successor's walk.
- `ForgedSeen` and `CapReached` persist: a restart keeps the first sighting and the reached standing; `flag -ids` keeps its own verdict (`ForgedVerdict` needs a broken identity).
- The refund mark is read before every attempt, so a stage resumed after a transient failure re-reads it; a refund inside the seconds between the read and the process is the chain's race.
- The stroke on the monitor: a nil line hits the configured program, the pausable ISM first; the retired seats go in a second transaction, best effort; `FlagSolana` reads at confirmed through one provider, which decides nothing a lying provider cashes.
- The hygiene: `AlarmEvery` daily on `mint:cap-ratio`, `send:refunded:<n>`, `admin:successor`; `gno:own-lag` raised and cleared at monitor.go:293-299; the alarms of G35 end with their cures.

## Verdict

Round 4's fixes hold at their lines, and the race suite is green. One medium: the dispatch index has no checkpoint, so a fresh install on a provider without the full history mutes the forged-credit rule for good — G29's stall by the other door, with no cure flag (G36). Three medium-lows at the new code's edges: a checkpoint trusts a forked trusted header and hlwatch never alarms its mismatch (G37), the gone-decision rule flags the validators' own cancel (G38), and the one-stroke flag dies whole on an unreadable line (G40). Six lows. Nothing above medium; fold G36–G38 and G40 before devnet.
