## Findings

### R31 — low — a migrate of a successor that never pulled page 0 strands the first predecessor's lock for good
Rule: PLAN §5.3 ("a range void or a second migrate is refused while pages still arrive"; builders "refuse nonsense early"; R28's reading in M1-NOTES M1g).
Files: realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1221–1229 (`DraftMigrate`: `mustNotBeImporting` only — `importing` is false until page 0's `Import` sets it at 1855), 1849–1853 (`Import` refuses once migrated), 1764–1770 (`Export` only after a migrate, to the named successor's call); realm/hlgnot/hlgnot.gno:467–474 and hlbubble.gno:413–420 (`Pull` is the named successor's alone); hlroute.gno:1224 (a path that is a route already is not refused at the draft: `AddRoute` panics at apply, flags.gno:322–324).
Scenario: hlgnot migrates to B (day 7). B's `Adopt` is never called — its deployer waits, or its caller gate is wrong — and a member proposes `migrate B to C`, C announced for GNOT: B is not importing, so the draft passes and applies a week on, `B.MigratedTo = C`. Now B's `Import` refuses (migrated), so `A.Pull` from B reverts with it, and C may not pull A (`A.MigratedTo == B`): the whole lock, every claim and every send record sit on A, which refuses everything, and no decision reaches them. A vote naming a path that is a route already dies the same way at `AddRoute` after its 7 days, pending until a cancel.
Fix: `DraftMigrate` and `apply(migrateParams)` refuse while the route has a predecessor and `imported == 0` (the realm passes `hlmailbox.Predecessor(pkgPath) != ""`, or `Env` gains it): "page 0 is not in yet"; `DraftMigrate` also refuses `hlmailbox.IsRoute(to)`. Test: a migrate of an un-pulled successor refused at both clocks.

### R32 — low — a successor enrolled before its page 0 sweeps every honest burn addressed to it
Rule: PLAN §5.3 ("the successor credits nothing by the verifier" until the last page), §5.4 ("a credited amount above the lock goes to the sweep queue").
Files: realm/hlgnot/hlgnot.gno:235–239 and hlbubble.gno:220–224 (`MustHaveImported` only when `m.Recipient != self`); realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:506–527 (`Credit`: `importing` is false before page 0, so `amount > s.Lock` — a lock of 0 — sweeps at 521), 1752–1756; realm/hlgov/hlgov.gno:539–556 (kind 6 accepts the successor the moment `AddRoute` ran).
Scenario: the migrate applies on day 7; GovDAO proposes kind 6 for B that day and it lands on Solana a week later; B's `Adopt` is still uncalled. Burns now name B as recipient: B is not importing and holds no lock, so every burn above B's own few sends is swept — owed, paying nobody — each cured only by a 7-day credit-by-id vote, one id a proposal. The relayer sees them delivered and moves on; the page lists a sweep queue the size of a week's burns.
Fix: in both routes' `Process`, apply `st.MustHaveImported()` to every message while `hlmailbox.Predecessor(pkgPath) != ""`, not only to one addressed elsewhere: the message waits undelivered and the relayer brings it again. Test: a burn to a successor before page 0 refused, taken after the last page.

### R33 — low — a refunded send's mark does not survive the next migration: no realm answers `refunded` for it afterwards
Rule: PLAN §5.3 (the send "marked `refunded` — a mark the monitors and `hlwatch` read and drop from in flight"); M1-NOTES M1g AQ2 ("the mark is readable: `send/<id>` …, `refunds/<from>` …").
Files: realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1820–1834 (`Export` walks every send, removes it from `sends` and `refunds`, and carries only the unrefunded ones), 1897–1899 (`Import` has no `Refunded`), 2070–2080 and 2090–2106 (`send/<id>` and `refunds/<from>` read `sends`/`refunds` only), 1943 and 2062 (`nRefunded`/`refundedTotal` stay); hlroute_test.gno:828–836 pins `RefundsPage(0) == "[]"` after the export.
Scenario: B refunded bob's 2,000 imported send; a year on B migrates to C. The page drops the refunded record: B's `send/<id>` answers null and its `refunds/0` `[]` while its json still says `refunded 1`; C never held it. A monitor installed fresh from a checkpoint (G30), or one that re-derives what is in flight from the mailbox's dispatches, finds no realm that calls the send refunded and carries 2,000 in flight for good — a standing slack or a standing alarm by the Go side's rule, with nothing on chain left to correct it.
Fix: carry refunded sends in the page (`SendItem.Refunded`; `Import` keeps the mark and the `refunds` index; `DraftRefund` already refuses `Refunded != 0`), or leave them on the predecessor — never drop the only record. Test: `send/<id>` answers `refunded ≠ 0` on the route that holds it after a second migration.

### R34 — low — a successor's meters are full at its first `Claim`: a migration pays a third cap into the 24 hours it closes
Rule: PLAN §5.4 ("both meters start empty at `Launch` … and after every unpause"; "at most two caps in the worst 24 hours"; "a forged day's mint before the flags is then bounded by … the payout meter's own bound").
Files: realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:302–308 (`New`: `meter.At = l.Now`, the deploy), 587–595 (`ResetMeters(now)` moves every meter's `At` to the time given), 1904–1915 (`meterView` the same), 1849–1900 (`Import` carries no meter and sets no `LaunchSeen`); realm/hlgnot/hlgnot.gno:126–132 and 281, hlbubble.gno:117–122 and 266 (`syncLaunch`: `LaunchedAt() != LaunchSeen (0)` → `ResetMeters(launchedAt)`, months in the past, so the refill fills every meter at once).
Scenario: a forger holding the signer keys reads the migrate decision's due time and watches for the successor's pull. The predecessor paid its two caps' worth on its last day; the successor imports, and its first `Claim` runs `syncLaunch`, which rewinds the bridge meter and every imported recipient's quarter to the launch time: all full. Four addresses draw a whole cap more at once — three caps in one 24-hour window — in the seconds before the watch's second poll flags; the page showed the meter full, so nobody alarmed.
Fix: `Import` of page 0 empties the bridge meter as of `now` (`s.meter.Level, At, Frac = 0, now, 0`) and sets `s.LaunchSeen` to hlmailbox's launch time (an argument, or the realm's `init` sets it), so a successor starts empty as a launch route does. Test: the successor's first `Claim` after the last page pays nothing.

## Checked and held

- Spec §5.1–§5.2, guide §5.6/§5.8, review 1 and 9: every function M1g added or changed derives identity from its own live `cur.Previous()` — `Process` walks the succession from the caller's pkgpath, never a parameter; `AddRoute` by `mustBeRoute`; `AnnounceSuccessor` and `Pull` by exact pkgpath; no secondary realm parameter in hl* or p/; the checker's one hit is realm/bubbletreasury, outside the bridge.
- Spec §5.5: pkgpath-keyed powers stay exact — `successorOf` walks `routes`' exact keys, so a `host#subpath` token is nobody's successor; `AddRoute` and `DraftMigrate` refuse a `#` path; `AnnounceSuccessor` refuses a sub-identity by `Subpath()` (pinned uverse.go:185) and any caller outside `gno.land/r/`.
- Spec §5.3, guide §5.7, review 6: the succession, the announced symbol, `former` and every flag copy are strings, ints or address arrays; no realm value in `routeRec`, `flag`, `Send`, `Snapshot` or any param; `env(cur)` is built per call in the executing frame and never stored.
- Spec §3.2/§4.1–§4.3, guide §2.1–§2.3: `copyFlags` allocates the mailbox's own `*flag` for the new path; a page's claims, sweeps and sends are value copies the successor allocates as its own (the `Imported` mark with them); every `Env` closure runs under the route that declared it, so `AddRoute` and `AnnouncedFor` are the route's own calls; `Pull` returns a fresh `*Snapshot` with no mutator.
- Spec §9, guide §6.1: a migrated or not-yet-imported route panics before `hlmailbox.Process` marks, so the mark never stands for a refused message; `mustFitRange`, the refund floor, a stale swap, an unannounced or other-route successor panic and hold the decision for a retry or a cancel; `AddRoute`'s refusal at apply reverts `MigratedTo` with it.
- Spec §11.3, guide §5.1/§5.1a, review 3/8: `SendJSON`, `RefundsPage`, `Former`, `Predecessor`, `Successor`, `SuccessorFor`, `Imported` return strings, copies or numbers; `st`, `routes`, `flags`, `successors`, `former` stay unexported; no exported function takes a func or interface value M1g added.
- The id across the pair (§5.1, §5.3): one `done` tree; the predecessor refuses before the mailbox after its migrate; a successor takes only once its last page is in; a hand credit on the successor for an id on a page still to come is "done already" (every claim's id is marked where it was credited); a message addressed to the successor is nobody else's; two hops tested.
- `MustHaveImported` (§5.3): `imported == 0 || importing` — before page 0 the lock is absent, between pages `Credit` refuses anyway, a launch route refuses such a message for good; `importing` runs from page 0's import to the page with `More == false`, and an empty predecessor ends it at page 0.
- `Send.Imported` and the refund (§5.3): set by `Import` alone, never by `Send`; `DraftRefund` refuses an own send and sums ≤ lock; `apply` re-sums only live imported unrefunded sends ≤ lock before paying and skips the rest with a count; `send/<id>` is one lookup on a validated id, `refunds/<from>` ≤100 by offset over an index GovDAO alone grows; `refundedTotal` is bounded by what the lock ever held.
- `AnnounceSuccessor` (§5.3): once per path, the symbol fixed at the first call (a second is refused, the route unchanged); `DraftMigrate` and the closure refuse "" and the other route's symbol; the announcement binds path → symbol and nothing of the code — a `private` gnomod stays §8.3's member check, as round 4 recorded; a re-upload whose init announces again fails on "announced itself already".
- `copyFlags` at the migrate's apply (§5.3): every standing flag of the migrating route, the seat's own on the new path, cleared there by the seat, by GovDAO's unpause on the successor's path (`routes.Has`) or by `clearSeat` on every route; the successor's epoch starts at 0 and its imported queues take it; a flag set on the predecessor after the copy guards a realm that refuses everything; the `|` separator sorts above every pkgpath byte, so one route's prefix never covers another's.
- `Pull` page 0 (§5.3, R16): the gate is the predecessor's own payouts flag, liftable on that path; later pages carry records only; the lock payment and the export revert together; a page pulled already pays nothing; `Export`'s `QueueNext` is final since nothing credits there after the migrate.
- `mustNotBeImporting` and `mustFitRange` (§5.3): the range draft and the migrate draft refuse while pages arrive; the list form stays open and names only what stands; the pre-count at `Decide` and `apply` precedes any void and costs ≤1,001 reads; a range drafted before page 0 is counted again when it would apply.
- r/hlgov (§13): `former` grows one per applied swap; `mustNotBeFormer` in kind 9's `check`, the swap's `check` and `Execute`, which also refuses a target that is the recipient already; `MaxDecisionBody` 512 at `proposeKind`, the cancel builder and `dispatch`, the worst body 163 bytes; `Redispatch` resends a body that passed; kinds 10 and 11 absent; `mustRaise` ≤ 2^32.
- Bounds and pages (review 10/11): `successors/<from>` and the page's successors line ≤20 with the count; `successorOf` ≤16 reads; `isRouteKey` over the routes, which grow by migration alone; every `Render` path M1g added parses its input and never panics (a `refunds` key always has its send; `CleanID` guards `send/<id>`).

## Verdict

Rounds 1–4's fixes hold, M1g's eight included (R27–R30, AQ1, AQ2, AQ5, F18); the realms meet the specification and the guide on every function M1g added or changed. Nothing critical, high or medium. Four lows, all at the migration's edges before page 0 or after the last: a migrate of a successor that never pulled strands the predecessor's lock for good (R31); a successor enrolled early sweeps honest burns against an empty lock (R32); a refunded send's mark vanishes at the next migration (R33); a successor's meters start full, a third cap in 24 hours (R34). Each is a guard of a few lines in p/hlroute or the route realms' `Process`.
