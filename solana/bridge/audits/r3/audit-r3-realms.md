## Findings

### R19 — medium — a sweep payment is by amount while entries are by id: a paid entry stays live, and the credit-by-id R11 added pays it a second time with the identity reading closed
Rule: PLAN §5.3 ("direct the sweep queue (pay a swept amount …)" beside "a sweep entry's id may be credited the same way, the entry dropped"); §5.9 (the counters the watch reads).
Files: realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1098–1104 (`DraftSweep` by amount), 1420–1428 (`apply`: `Sweep`, `Owed`, `Lock` fall; no entry is touched), 1469–1478 (`creditByVote`: an unvoided entry's full amount leaves `Sweep` and `Owed`, no floor), 719–726 (`voidSweep` the same), 1697–1706 and 1711–1717 (the page lists a paid entry as it did before); M1-NOTES M1d's R11 reading.
Scenario: alice's 60-GNOT burn lands addressed to r/hlfees and is swept (entry E; Sweep = Owed = 60). GovDAO pays her — `sweep 60 to alice` — after 7 days: Sweep 0, Owed 0, Lock −60; E still stands in `sweeps`, on the page and at `claim/<id>`. A member later proposes the other cure the realms' comments name, `credit E to alice for 60`: `creditByVote` finds E unvoided, takes 60 from Sweep and Owed (−60 each), drops E and queues a 60 claim the meters pay. Alice holds 120 of a 60 burn, the lock is 60 short of the supply, and `lock = supply + owed + voided − lost` holds with owed negative: the watch flags nothing. A void of E after the payment drives the counters negative the same way and trips the mistaken-void alarm.
Fix: settle the queue by id — `DraftSweep(ids, to)` pays the named live entries' sum (≤ `Lock`) and drops them from `sweeps` and `credits` as `creditByVote` does; the by-amount form goes; `voidSweep` and `creditByVote` refuse an entry whose amount exceeds `Sweep`. Test: pay, then credit the same id.

### R20 — medium — the paged migration wedges on a credit between pages: `Import` places a predecessor's claim at base + old seq while the successor's own credits take the next seq, and the collision panics on every retry
Rule: PLAN §5.3 (migrate "in pages of 500 records the successor pulls"); M1-NOTES R13 ("between pages the successor owes claims it does not hold yet", "whatever order the pages arrive in").
Files: realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1614–1621 (`q.base = q.next` once; `Seq = q.base + c.Seq`; `q.next` only raised), 1626–1628 (`items.Has` → panic), 515–530 (`enqueue`: `q.next++`), 1523–1531 (`Export`: `page > exported` refused, so the failed page is the only one offered); hlgnot.gno:449–463 and hlbubble.gno:397–411 (`Pull` pays and exports in the successor's transaction).
Scenario: a busy route migrates; page 0 carries the counters, the lock and 500 claims; bob's old seq 1 lands at 1 (base 0, q.next 1). Before page 1 the relayer delivers a burn to bob on the successor — `enqueue` gives it seq 2, open. Page 1 carries bob's old seq 2 → place 2 → `items.Has` → panic; the transaction reverts, the predecessor's `exported` with it, so page 1 panics on every retry. The lock moved with page 0; every claim and send on later pages stays in a predecessor whose every function refuses, and no decision reaches them. A credit by vote or a restore to bob between pages does the same.
Fix: `Export` writes each claim's recipient counter (the predecessor's `q.next`, final once migrated) into the page as `Claim.QueueNext`; `Import` sets `q.next = max(q.next, q.base + QueueNext)` on first seeing the recipient, so the successor's own credits land above every place a page can claim. Test: a credit between two pages carrying one recipient's consecutive seqs.

### R21 — low — the at-once `rotate seats` removal vacates whoever holds the seat at execution: R17's sibling, unguarded
Rule: PLAN §5.3 as v30 states it (AUDIT.md round 2: "Seat removals name the key held at creation and skip a changed occupant").
Files: realm/hlmailbox/gov.gno:329–351 (`NewRotateSeatsRequest`: `removals` → `applySeats` with no holder recorded; `before` only for the queued path), 353–365 (`applySeats`: `seats[n] != "" → clearSeat; seats[n] = ""`), 312–322 (the text names the holder at creation and nothing checks it); contrast flags.gno:283–295 and gov.gno:426–435.
Scenario: `rotate seats: remove seat 3 (g1old)` is proposed against a key suspected compromised. While it is voted, a queued replacement that put g1new in seat 3 executes. The removal's closure takes the at-once path; `applySeats` finds seat 3 held, clears g1new's flags (the meters restart if a payouts flag stood) and vacates it: the honest replacement is undone without a vote, and the seat is empty for 7 days more. R17 closed this for the void builder and the unpause; this path names no holder.
Fix: record `seats[n]` for each removal in `rotateSeatsParams` at creation; in `applySeats` skip a removal whose seat no longer holds that address and say so in the note, as `VacateSeat` does. Test: a replacement landing between a removal's proposal and its execution.

### R22 — low — a list void whose id another decision voided in between panics at execution instead of skipping, blocking the whole proposal, its unpause option and seat removals with it
Rule: PLAN §5.3 ("a change that fails its at-once test there is queued 7 days, not refused"; the void "is made once, per id, in the transaction that executes the proposal").
Files: realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1226–1247 (`Decide`'s list branch calls `void`/`voidSweep` unconditionally), 672–702 (`void` panics on Forged, on Withheld-as-withheld, on Done-as-withheld), 719–722, 1395–1405 (`apply`'s list the same); contrast 1199–1223 and 1379–1393 (the range form skips and counts).
Scenario: two guardians flag overlapping ids under a flood; two members propose void lists that share id X. The first executes: X forged. The second executes: `Decide` finds X flagged and calls `void`, which panics "forged already"; the proposal — its other ids, its `andUnpause`, its seat removals — fails, survives every retry and lapses on day 14, during which payouts stay flagged. The same list queued (X unflagged at execution) panics at `Execute` 7 days later and stays pending until a cancel. The list was checked only when the proposal was made.
Fix: in `Decide`'s list branch and `apply`'s, skip and count a claim or entry not `voidable`/`sweepVoidable` as it stands — the range form's rule — noting `n skipped`. Test: two lists sharing an id.

### R23 — low — the range cap counts the credits a void can take, the walk covers every credit in the range: the natural repeated `since:` cleanup of a flood stops executing near the eighth proposal
Rule: PLAN §5.3 (the 1,000-credit cap, "split the range by heights", R14's reading in M1-NOTES).
Files: realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:996–1015 (`DraftVoid`: `n++` only when voidable, the walk unbounded), 745–765 (`eachInRange` loads every credit, a claim and maybe a sweep entry each), 1199–1223 (`Decide`'s at-once walk over the whole closed range), 1379–1393 (`apply` walks it again).
Scenario: 9,000 forged claims under a guardian's `since:H` flag. GovDAO's cleanup is `void as forged since:H`, repeated: each draft counts 1,000 voidable and passes, but `Decide`'s walk covers every credit from H to the closing height, the already-forged ones included, so the k-th proposal walks ≈k×1,000 credits at ≈0.35M gas each; near the eighth the at-once walk passes the 3B block ceiling, the closure runs out of gas and the proposal lapses. The refusal's advice, "split by heights", needs the last voided height, which nothing prints. A void as withheld over a range of dust entries (never voidable as withheld) walks them all the same.
Fix: count every credit the scan visits toward `MaxRangeClaims`, voidable or not; write the last height voided into the decision's note so the next `heights:` range starts after it. Test: a since-range over 1,000 forged plus 2,000 open credits.

### R24 — low — the route's page counts waiting claims by walking every waiting recipient; a flood of fee-sized claims to fresh addresses kills the page a member is told to read
Rule: review guide §10–§11 (bounded, attacker-independent `Render`); PLAN §8.3 (the page a member reads), §5.4 ("the realm renders every waiting claim and its position").
Files: realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1737–1744 (`waitingCount` iterates `waiting` whole), 1685 (`Figures` calls it before the capped list), 281–282 (`nOpen`, the same number by R12's invariant); hlgnot.gno:503, hlbubble.gno:444.
Scenario: with the leaked keys a forger delivers claims of the fee's size to 25,000 fresh addresses (≈2,250 GNOT of `Process` gas; each must be ≥ the fee, so dust will not do). `Render("")` — the page §8.3 names and the launch notice links — loads every `waiting` node under the 3B query ceiling and fails until the claims are paid or voided; `json`, `claim/<id>` and `claims/<from>` (which stop at 100) survive. R6 capped the lists; the count walk came with R13's exact ring.
Fix: `Figures` takes the total from `s.nOpen`; keep `waitingCount` for the tests only. Test: 100 lines and the count with many recipients, by `nOpen`.

### R25 — low — `DraftMigrate` accepts a path with no realm behind it: a mistyped successor strands the lock for good or hands it to whoever may deploy at that path
Rule: PLAN §5.3 (migrate names "a successor realm"; builders "refuse nonsense early"; "no one holds an admin key"), §8.3.
Files: realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1122–1128 (prefix, `#` and self only), 1440–1442 (`apply`: `MigratedTo` set, the route registered, no way back), 380–384 (every function refuses after); hlgnot.gno:449–463 (`Pull` pays the whole lock to `chain.PackageAddress(path)`); flags.gno:300–310.
Scenario: GovDAO votes `migrate to gno.land/r/g1leu…/hlgnot2` with one character wrong. Seven days later the closure sets `MigratedTo` and registers the path; the route refuses everything from then on and no decision undoes it. If the path is deployable, its namespace owner — the operator's key for `gno.land/r/g1leu…/` — deploys whatever code it likes there, calls `Pull` and receives the lock: the admin key the plan says nobody holds. If it is not deployable, the lock is stranded.
Fix: a successor announces itself — `hlmailbox.AnnounceSuccessor(cur)` records the caller's exact pkgpath (a sub-identity refused as in `RegisterVerifier`) — and `DraftMigrate` and the closure refuse a path not announced. Test: a migrate to an unannounced path.

### R26 — low — a hand credit is not bounded by the lock (R7's sibling), and a claim's turn can ask for more than the lock holds, which reverts `Claim` for every recipient
Rule: PLAN §5.4 ("a credited amount above the lock goes to the sweep queue"; one claim "is bound by its recipient's quarter").
Files: realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1077–1094 (`DraftCredit`: `amount > 0` only), 1453–1484 (`creditByVote` queues it), 496 (`Credit` sweeps above the lock), 607–627 (`Claim`: `avail` ≤ cap/4 where cap = max(floor, Lock/10), never ≤ `Lock`), 633–635 (`env.Pay`); hlgnot.gno:278–280 (`SendCoins`: a shortfall is a panic).
Scenario: a member credits by hand 8,000 GNOT for an id whose true burn was 800. The identity opens by 7,200 and the watch flags as for a forgery — but the lock is 2,000, under the 10,000 floor whose quarter is 2,500. At the recipient's turn `Claim` asks `Pay` for 2,500 against a 2,000 balance: `SendCoins` fails, the call reverts, the cursor with it, and every later `Claim` meets that recipient first until GovDAO voids the claim. The same turn arises after a forgery drained part of the lock while honest claims stand above it.
Fix: `DraftCredit` and `creditByVote` refuse `amount > s.Lock` (R7's rule); in `Claim`, `if avail > s.Lock { avail = s.Lock }` so a turn never asks for more than the lock holds and a dry lock is a skip, not a revert. Test: a claim larger than the lock under the floor.

## Verdict

Rounds 1 and 2's fixes hold, X1 and X2 too; the realms meet the specification and the guide. Nothing critical or high. Two mediums: a sweep payment by amount leaves its entry live, so R11's credit-by-id pays it again while the identity reads closed (R19); the paged migration wedges on a credit between pages (R20). Six lows: R17's missed rotate-seats sibling, a list void's panic on a changed id, a cap counting voidable while walking all, the page's recipient walk, a migrate to an undeployed path, a turn above the lock. All in p/hlroute and gov.gno; fix before the freeze.
