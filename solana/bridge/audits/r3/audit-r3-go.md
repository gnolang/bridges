## Findings

### G22 — medium — the gap rule cannot fire: `Consistent` calls a complete scan "behind", for good
agent/record.go:300-306 (`behind:` whenever `ScanSlot < snapshotSlot && Count <= records`), 186-209 (`Sync` bounds the fold at `maxSlot` but leaves `ScanSlot` at the last folded transaction's slot, below the snapshot unless a transaction landed in that very slot); agent/monitor_sol.go:70-78 (the "behind" answer clears `header:gap` and drops its sighting); cmd/hlwatch/main.go:457-461 prints the same text.
Scenario: after every successful `Sync` the record is complete up to `cs.Slot`, yet `ScanSlot` is the last transaction's slot, below `cs.Slot` on nearly every poll. A client whose counter is one above the rebuilt record — a header the watch never saw: provider 1 omitting it from the listing (the listing is one provider's by design; the counter was the cross-check), an instruction form the scan does not model — reads "behind: N of N+1 entries rebuilt" on every poll for good: `header:gap` cleared, no alarm, no flag, hlwatch saying "behind", never "an unfillable gap"; the omitted header is never held against the own node either. §5.9's gap flag is dead; only `Count > records` and a hash mismatch at equal counts can fire. `TestRecordSyncStopsAtTheSnapshotSlot` lands its transaction at the snapshot's own slot, so it never sees this.
Fix: at the end of a `Sync` that folded every listed transaction at or below `maxSlot` without error, set `r.ScanSlot = max(r.ScanSlot, maxSlot)` (a complete scan stands at the snapshot); keep "behind" for a scan that errored midway. Test: a complete scan with `Count < records` answers "an unfillable gap".

### G23 — medium — a failed re-read makes a foreign decision: both Solana objects flagged on a transient error, never re-examined
agent/views_sol.go:567-578 (`body == nil` at H → `govBody(…, 0)`; on `err != nil` the number is called foreign and `checked = n`); agent/monitor_sol.go:204-211 (`flagSolana(…, adminToo)` per finding, no `persisted`); views_sol.go:464-471 and cmd/hlwatch/main.go:761-763 (`st.Foreign` appended to the state and kept).
Scenario: GovDAO's decision n is dispatched after the monitor's height H and delivered before step 7 reads the admin state — the lag G15 covered. The re-read at the head is one more POST to the own node; a closed keep-alive connection ("EOF"), a 30 s timeout or a rate limit on that one call returns an error, read as "no body": "foreign decision: the admin program recorded number n, r/hlgov's counter is n−1". The watch key flags the pausable ISM and the admin program at once (deliveries stop, the 7-day group defers), `DecisionsChecked` moves past n so nothing re-examines it, and every guardian's hlwatch keeps a FOREIGN line for good. The cure is a hand-cleared watch flag or an unpause decision 24 hours after its delivery.
Fix: in `foreignFindings`, when the head re-read errors, `return out, checked, err` (the poll is skipped, `checked` stays at n−1); gate the monitor's foreign flag through `persisted("decision:foreign", cutFailAfter)` as the record's mismatch is. Test: an erroring head re-read yields no finding.

### G24 — low — the signer's status walks `s.index` while the loop writes it: G5's crash, on the loopback endpoint
agent/signer.go:694-699 (`Status()` ranges over `s.index` under `s.mu`), 524 and 470 (`record` and `indexScan` write `s.index[id]` and `rec.disputed` with no lock), 535 and 567 (`s.unsigned` likewise).
Scenario: the dispute counter M3f added to `Status()` is a `range` over the index; the mutex guards the status side only, the run loop writes the map outside it (round 2's `len()` reads were benign, a range is not). An operator's scraper polling `/v1/status` every second overlaps a `record()` write once in some 10⁵ dispatches: Go's runtime aborts with "concurrent map iteration and map write", systemd restarts the signer, the index is rebuilt from the signing cursor. Rare, loopback-only, a liveness blip — but the class G5 closed, reopened by the fix for G17; `go test -race` sees nothing because no test reads the status concurrently.
Fix: count the disputed records in `checkIdentity` after `indexScan` and store the number under `s.mu` beside `verdictSlot` (the status reads it), or take `s.mu` around every write to `s.index`, `rec.disputed` and `s.unsigned`.

### G25 — low — the URL redaction stops short of hlsol and of hlwatch's status page
cmd/hlsol/audit.go:54 (every `rpc> curl -s <req.URL>` line prints the provider URL whole), 95 (`hlsol audit of <url>`); cmd/hlsol/main.go (no `log.SetOutput(agent.RedactWriter…)`: `check(err)` at 402-406 prints solana-go errors, URL included, on any failed send, balance or audit); cmd/hlwatch/main.go:102, 432, 439, 442 (`status` prints `SolErr`, `Err` and `Unconfirmed` to stdout unredacted; `watch` redacts through the Alarmer only).
Scenario: §5.6 now says "no provider URL with its token in any alarm, log or status page". A member runs `hlsol audit -rpc https://…/?api-key=K` as §5.8 asks and posts the log to the members' channel to show the PASS: a hundred curl lines carry the key. A guardian's `hlwatch status` under a provider hiccup prints "not read: … rpc call getAccountInfo() on https://…/v2/KEY: …" into whatever they paste. The operator's machines are covered; the outsiders' tools, the ones meant to be shared, are not.
Fix: `log.SetOutput(agent.RedactWriter{W: os.Stderr})` in hlsol's main; print `agent.Redact(req.URL.String())` (or a `$RPC` placeholder) in the curl line and the header; `fmt.Print(agent.Redact(v.text()))` in hlwatch's status.

### G26 — low — hlwatch's kind 8/9 program reads rest on one provider, unmarked
cmd/hlwatch/main.go:257-272 (`authorityOf` reads a programdata or buffer from `w.providers[0]` alone), 283-330 (the verdicts "immutable", "MUTABLE", "staged" appended to the proposal line), 829 (`SeatOnSolana` from provider 0 too).

The K16 check, the one a member reads before voting a kind 9, does not: a lying first provider answers "successor … immutable (kind 9 can execute)" for a mutable successor; GovDAO votes; the admin program spends the kind 9 and the transfer is re-proposed after a burn and a second vote — a wasted vote and a week, nothing stolen. ### G27 — low — hygiene after M3f: bookkeeping that outlives its decision, alarms that fire once or churn, a disputed record that waits for ever, a case-sensitive realm refusal
agent/executor_sol.go:107-179 (`Ready`, `Attempts`, `Extends` and the `execute:extend:` alarm are forgotten only in this relayer's own success branch, 174-175: a decision executed by the other relayer, by `hlrecover execute` or closed by a cancel leaves `solexec:`/`solext:` entries in the state file and a standing extend alarm until restart); agent/relayer.go:333-335, relayer_sol.go:408-410, executor_sol.go:153-154 (`parked:` is raised only when `a.fail` first parks, so the webhook hears a parked message once, not hourly as the Alarmer's contract and §5.7's "alert" read); agent/monitor.go:261-289, 321, 384-387 (`persist` keys `solana:providers`, `gno:own`, `cut` never drop their first sighting on success: after one two-minute spell every later single-poll failure alarms at once, and with two real providers under a header every tick `readClientBoth` disagrees on `Records` a few polls in ten); agent/monitor.go:818-825 (`stalls` counts a disputed record as waiting: a lying second sight whose `Sig` names no transaction stays disputed and raises `age` for good); cmd/hlsol/main.go:343 (`realmOf` compares the bech32 string exactly: an upper-case `-to` of a realm's address passes, and the burn is swept).
Scenario: a parked message's one post is missed in a busy channel and the message waits for days; a burst fills the monitor's channel with `solana:providers` alarm/clear pairs; the operator's status shows an extend alarm for a decision long gone.
Fix: in `solExecutes`, forget every `solexec:`/`solext:` key and its alarms whose decision is not in `pending`; re-raise `parked:` on each hourly retry's failure; `delete(m.seen, key)` beside each `Clear`; skip disputed records in `stalls`; `strings.ToLower` in `realmOf`.

## Checked and held

- The signer's deferral: the batch stops at the first dispatch above `verdictSlot`; under a steady flow the next held cut's slot (both providers' finalized reads, the lower taken) passes it within a round; a round without a verdict stops signing after one round (`verdictMaxAge` = 2 × Every) and halts after an hour; a dispute is a round with no verdict, so a provider disputing everything halts its signer within the hour (§5.6's bound), and a dispute clears only when both providers show the recorded transaction's slot, bytes and accounts alike.
- The monitor's bounded scan: the cursor stands before the first transaction above `cs.Slot`, which the next listing (`Until` = the cursor) names again; a disputed record is out of inflight, credited-after, the diverted check and the void match; `header:mismatch` is sticky by design and flags only after two minutes; a mismatch found is re-flagged at most once a minute through the watch key.
- Redaction on the machines: every alarm, note, status snapshot (`statusBox.publish`, the signer's handler) and log line passes `Redact`; the e2e's status dumps are those snapshots; a parked entry's `err` reaches the status redacted (the state file keeps it raw, mode 0600); the relayer's, monitor's and updater's `-listen` refuse a non-loopback address, the signer's public listener needs `-tls-cert` off loopback and its status listener is loopback-only.
- The extend: `ExtendBy` is the whole excess at or above 10,240 or exactly the headroom below 10 MiB; one instruction; the executor's or `hlrecover execute`'s payer funds the rent; the cost is alarmed before the send of the same tick; the backup, after its offset, finds `add == 0`.
