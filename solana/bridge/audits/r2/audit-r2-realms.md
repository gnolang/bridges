## Findings

### R11 — high — the sweep queue has no void: one forged sweep entry keeps the identity open for good
realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:453–473 (`Credit`: a burn below the fee, to a realm or non-gno recipient, or above the lock becomes `sweeps[id] = amount` and `Owed += amount`), 626–668 (`void`/`voidable` take a `*Claim` only), 952–966 and 1289–1291 (`DraftCredit`/`creditByVote` refuse an id in `sweeps` as "done already"), 969–975 and 1238–1246 (a sweep payment moves coins and lowers `Lock` with `Owed`); realm/hlgnot/hlgnot.gno:245–246 and hlbubble.gno:230–231 promise "until GovDAO credits it by id", which no path does.
Scenario: with two signer keys (§1's leak ending) a forger delivers one message of amount `Lock + 1`, or addressed to r/hlfees — `Process` costs ≈0.09 GNOT and needs no Solana burn. It is swept: `Owed` rises by the amount and nothing on gno lowers it without paying — a void takes claims only, a credit by id refuses a sweep id, and a sweep payment lowers `Lock` with `Owed`, so the right side still exceeds the lock. §5.9's identity stays broken by that amount; the honest signers, and any rotated-in set, refuse to sign for good; only a successor's hand-written `Adopt` could drop it.
Fix: make a sweep entry a record `{Amount, Height, By, Voided}` indexed in `credits`; let the void (list and range, the flag test, the 7-day split) take a sweep id as forged — `Voided = true; Sweep -= a; Owed -= a` — and the restore reverse it after 7 days; let `creditByVote` replace a sweep id (drop the entry, queue the named claim); bound a sweep payment by `min(Sweep, Lock)`.

### R12 — high — voided claims stay in their recipient's queue; a flood of them, once voided, stops every payout of the route
realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:493–504 (`oldestOpen` walks `q.items` to the first open claim), 626–655 (`void` changes the state and never removes the item), 559–562 (`Claim` sets `s.cursor`, then walks), 612–619 (`finish` walks again), 687–706 (`restore`), 1320–1349 (`Export` walks the same items).
Scenario: the forger of R11 delivers 2,000 forged claims to one address (≈180 GNOT of gas). GovDAO voids them by range — the cure — and each stays in `q.items` as a tombstone ahead of anything open. Every visit of that queue now loads about three stored objects a tombstone (≈177,000 gas): the relayers' 45M-gas `Claim` dies at ≈250 tombstones, a 3B block at ≈17,000. The transaction reverts, the cursor with it, so the next `Claim` visits the same queue first: payouts stop for every recipient; past 17,000 no gas limit passes it, and `Export` walks them too.
Fix: `void` removes the claim from `q.items` (and from `waiting` when none open remain); `restore` re-inserts it at its `Seq`; `Export` walks `claims` (so voided and paid claims migrate as well) and `Import` queues only `Open` ones. Test: 300 voided claims ahead of one open, then `Claim`.

### R13 — medium — `Pull`/`Export` is one transaction over every send, queue and sweep entry ever recorded; the migration stops working within months
realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1320–1349 (`Export` iterates `queues`, `sweeps` and every `sends` record), 1353–1384 (`Import` writes each in the same transaction), 1440–1462 (`eachWaiting`/`waitingCount` iterate every queue, empty ones included); realm/hlgnot/hlgnot.gno:447–461, hlbubble.gno:395–409 (`Pull` is one call).
Scenario: nothing marks a send delivered, so `sends` keeps every send ever; a record costs ≈177,000 gas to read and ≈340,000 for the successor's write. At the planned 50 transfers a day the snapshot passes the block's 3B ceiling after ≈4 months (≈12 days at 500 a day) and `Pull` can never execute: §5.3's migration — the cure for a stranded or outgrown route — is gone. `Queue` objects never die either, so the route's page and `claims/<from>` walk every recipient ever made, under the same 3B query ceiling.
Fix: page the export — `Pull(cur, from int)` returns the counters and pays the lock on page 0 and up to 500 records a page by key cursor (claims, sweeps, sends), the successor importing page by page; keep a `nonEmpty` tree of queues with items and walk it in `eachWaiting`, `waitingCount` and `Export`.

### R14 — medium — a range void of a thousand claims costs ≈0.4B gas, twice; a range past ≈7,000 can never execute
realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:672–684 (`eachInRange`), 899–907 (`DraftVoid` scans only to the first voidable claim), 1068–1082 (the at-once walk inside GovDAO's transaction), 1210–1219 (`Execute` walks the frozen range again).
Scenario: per claim the walk reads two `credits` nodes and about four uncached `claims` nodes (59,000 gas each) and writes the claim (24,000): ≈0.4M gas, so a thousand claims cost ≈0.4B (≈0.4 GNOT at 0.001 ugnot/gas) in the executing transaction and the same again at `Execute`. Near 7,000 the 3B block ceiling is reached: the closure runs out of gas, a panic, so the proposal survives every retry and lapses on its 14th day, after which GovDAO re-proposes split ranges — after a flood, exactly when the range form was wanted.
Fix: `DraftVoid` counts the voidable claims in its scan and refuses a range above `MaxRangeClaims = 1,000` (GovDAO splits by heights); or `Execute` applies up to 1,000 a call and keeps a height cursor in the params. Measure on the devnet build.

### R15 — low — the kind-12 builder is not tied to the routes' per-message maximum, so sends between the two maxima strand
bridge/local/hlgov_mintlimits.gno:18–30 (bounds: `max ≥ 1`, `dayCap ≥ max`, the 17-byte body right); realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:768–777 (`DraftMaximum` knows nothing of the ISM's limit); the admin program parses the body with no bound of its own (admin-program/src/kinds.rs:171–176).
Scenario: GovDAO sets GNOT's mint limit to 10,000 a message while r/hlgnot's maximum stays 25,000 (or raises the route's maximum above the ISM's): every send between the two locks the sender's coins, the pausable ISM refuses the delivery for good, and the only cure is a refund by id, 7 days after a vote.
Fix: r/hlgov imports the routes and refuses `max < hlgnot.Maximum()` (token 0) or `max < hlbubble.Maximum()` (token 1) at creation and execution; M1-NOTES orders a raise: the ISM's limit first, the route's maximum after.

### R16 — low — a sweep payment or a refund pays coins under a standing `payouts` flag
realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1238–1257 (`apply` for sweep and refund calls `env.Pay`); realm/hlgnot/hlgnot.gno:438–443 and hlbubble.gno:386–391 (`Execute` checks `mustBeLive` only; `Claim` alone checks the flag).
Scenario: the watch flags `credits` and `payouts` on an identity break — §5.9: nothing credited or paid under a suspect verifier. A sweep payment or refund queued a week earlier still executes and moves coins out of the lock while the flag stands; a forged above-lock sweep entry GovDAO was talked into paying is the case that matters.
Fix: in both realms' `Execute`, when the decision is a sweep or a refund, `mustNotBePaused(hlmailbox.FnPayouts)` — a panic, so the decision waits for the unpause.

### R17 — low — a seat removal in a void or an unpause names the seat, not the key it meant
realm/hlgnot/hlgnot.gno:384–388 and hlbubble.gno:342–346 (only "non-empty" is checked), realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1101–1103 (`env.RemoveSeat(seat)`), realm/hlmailbox/gov.gno:390–397 and 416–421 (`removeSeat` likewise).
Scenario: a void naming "remove seat 3" is proposed against a compromised guardian; while it is voted a queued `rotate seats` lands and seat 3 holds a fresh honest key; the void vacates the new guardian, flags and all, while the compromised key was already gone.
Fix: record the seat's address at creation (`RemoveSeats` as `seat:address`, `unpauseParams.removeAddr`) and skip a seat whose occupant changed, saying so in the note.

### R18 — low — `sync sequence` is unbounded above; past 2^64−1 the counter wraps onto numbers the admin program recorded
realm/hlgov/hlgov.gno:548–555 (`mustRaise` refuses only a non-raise), 561–576, 200–208 (`n := last + 1` with no overflow check).
Scenario: a mistyped sync to 18446744073709551615 passes; the next decision takes number 0 (`last + 1` wraps), later ones 1, 2, … — numbers the admin program recorded, so it ignores every decision until a recovery kind 10. Short of the wrap, a sync far above the admin program's next number makes every decision undeliverable (§5.7: only the next expected number is delivered) until the validators' recovery.
Fix: `mustRaise` refuses `n > last + 1 + (1<<32)`, kind 10's own bound (§13).

## Checked and held

- Authorization: every mutator takes a live `cur`; `Process` only by the message's recipient realm; `MarkDone`/`ClearFlags`/`VacateSeat`/`AddRoute` route-only by pkgpath; `SetFlag`/`ClearFlag` seat-only; `Withdraw` treasurer-only, everything held, to itself; `Pull` successor-only; builders are pure (nothing recorded at creation, so no spam); every closure runs from the proxy's frame (`mustBeDAO` holds under v1.2.0's `SimpleExecutor`), re-checks `live`, the deadline and its own preconditions; a panic reverts GovDAO's Accepted mark, so a proposal survives a retry, and the 14-day error ends it.
- `Process`: version, destination and recipient in the mailbox, origin and router (the old one for `Delay`) in the route, `done.Set` before `Verify`, a `Verify` error a panic, id = keccak of the whole message, so no replay across routes, verifiers or deployments; `Dispatch` and `Reemit` emit §13's seven attributes in order from hlmailbox's own frame with `hash` = sha256 of the bytes.
- Signers: every signature in the metadata must verify, in increasing index order, and the signers that signed meet the rule; the digest binds origin, hook, root, index and id; a pure removal is at once only under the live rule pruned, a strict subset and the active verifier's `Satisfiable` in the executing transaction; any rule change, addition or lowered threshold waits 7 days; a queued rotation applies only against the set it was decided on.
- Seats and flags: keyed by seat, cleared on removal or replacement, at most two guardian seats per proposal on all three paths, id lists ≤256, watch keys name nothing, a `since` range covers the verifier's credits only and a `heights` range hand credits too, the epoch bumps only when a standing payouts flag is cleared by an unpause, a removal or the void option — never by a key's own clear.
- Clocks: `made` is the builder call's block time; every at-once test is decided at execution against the live state; a failed unpause is spent; `Launch` is one-shot under the 14-day deadline (a second returns the error, so it is denied), every other builder but cancel refuses before it, and both meters empty at `Launch` and at every epoch.
- Meters: exact refills carrying the remainder, never above the ceiling, the cap re-read per turn so it only falls with the lock, a quarter per recipient, the fee whole as the first installment and at most the cap floor's quarter, turns by cursor with ≤4n visits, the per-message maximum and the minimum at send, the cap floor never below the launch floor, the maximum never below the launch minimum.
- The identity: every transition of send, credit, sweep, claim, void (open/withheld/done), restore (both), credit by vote, sweep payment, refund, export and import keeps `lock = sent − paid` and moves `supply + owed + voided − lost` only where the plan says; no double refund, sweep, void, restore, credit or migrate — each is re-checked at `apply`.
- GNOT envelope: exact `{amount + fee ugnot}` read with `unsafe.OriginSend`, `IsUserCall` then `AssertOriginCall`, the fee spent with an OriginSend banker, payouts and refunds with fresh RealmSend bankers; BUBBLE through the caller's own allowance with a balance-delta check; no callback runs during any coin or token move, so no reentrancy.
- r/hlgov: kinds 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and the stand-in 12 match §13 byte for byte (the salt, both cancel forms, kind 5's count and `seat ‖ pubkey` pairs, the 17-byte kind 12); kind 4 refused; kind 6 for the local domain route-only; kind 8 never the recipient; kind 10 and 11 absent; `sync sequence` raise-only at creation and execution from the compiled start; a re-dispatch keeps the body and salt; hlfees' treasurer swap waits 7 days and cancels at once.
- Storage: `sent`, `done`, `claims`, `sweeps`, `sends` grow only at a payer's cost; flags, seats and the decision pages are bounded; the pages a member reads cap their lists at 100 (R6); a registered-verifier flood would need a realm deployment per entry under a 3B query ceiling, not worth a finding.
- Migration: in-flight burns addressed to the predecessor are refused by it once the migrate applies and cannot be taken by the successor (the recipient differs); the successor's credit by id is the cure, as §5.3 intends; a second `Pull` exports nothing and pays nothing.
- Tests' blind spots: no sweep void (none exists, R11); no `Claim` behind voided claims or after a mass void (R12); no migration past a handful of records (R13); no range void above six claims (R14); no `Process` refused under a `credits` flag; the real hlverifier's `Satisfiable` never drives an at-once rotation (the realm tests use a copy of the rule); hlbubble's void, restore, refund and migrate only through hlgnot; a void's seat vacated after a replacement (R17); the first `Claim` after `Launch` with a queue made in between resets that meter to the launch time (one quarter at most, inside the bridge meter — not a finding).

## Verdict

Round 1's ten fixes hold. Nothing critical. Two highs, both reached from the plan's own leak ending (two signer keys): a forged sweep entry no decision can drop keeps the identity open and the honest signers stopped (R11), and the void that cures a forged flood leaves tombstones that stop every payout (R12). Two mediums are liveness: the one-shot migration export outgrows a block within months (R13), and the range void's gas, now bounded at ≈0.4M a claim with a ceiling near 7,000 (R14). The lows are guards. R11–R13 are small changes inside p/hlroute; fix them before the realms are frozen.
