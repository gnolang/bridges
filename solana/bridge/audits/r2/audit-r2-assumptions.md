**F6 (medium, latent) — the Go side's staging transactions exceed Solana's 1,232-byte packet past small sets.** `NextSetChunk = 25` makes a 1,244-byte transaction (1,284 with the ComputeLimit instruction `NextSetTxs` prepends); and `UpdateTxs` stages every precommit in one `StagePrecommitsIx` beside the init, which overflows at 18 validators (17 with the budget). Smallest change: `NextSetChunk = 23`; stage precommits in chunks through the offset the instruction already takes; a test that serializes every builder's transaction for a 60-validator set and asserts ≤ 1,232 bytes.

**F7 (low) — the "10 KB per call" rule is not the loader's.** `can_data_be_resized` bounds an extension only by the 10 MiB maximum and the transaction's resize delta; the CLI's `program extend` sends one instruction with the whole amount; the 10,240-per-instruction rule (r1-49) is the BPF `realloc`'s. Smallest change: §6.5 says "at least 10 KB, one extension a block"; `NeedsExtend` may answer the whole excess (rounded up to ≥ 10,240, or the headroom) so a fix grows in one block; r1-50's "not active" is corrected.

**F8 (low) — mainnet's slot is 0.22 s, not 0.4.** Performance samples and epoch starts agree (26.2 h an epoch since epoch 1053). §13's "24 h ≈ 216,000 slots, 7 days ≈ 1,512,000, 2 epochs = 4 days", §2's "epoch ≈ 2 days" and §7.2's griefing cost (4–9 SOL a day) are 1.8× off; no rule reads a slot count (the Clock measures every delay).
