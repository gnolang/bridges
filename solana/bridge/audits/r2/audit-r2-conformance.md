## 4. Findings (the rules implemented differently or not at all)

**K13 — medium — §5.8: `hlwatch` shows the supply identity "and alarm[s] its holder directly".** cmd/hlwatch/main.go:306-317 prints each route's verdict in `status`, but `watch`'s `diff` (:511-661) compares flags, decisions, proposals, pauses, seats, holds, floors, the settled set, limits, recoveries, quarantine, header mismatch, counters, foreign decisions and voids — never a route's identity. A guardian running `hlwatch watch` is told nothing when BUBBLE's verdict flips to BROKEN; only the operator's monitors (whose flag then shows as a gno flag alarm) would tell them. Smallest fix: in `diff`, compare `a.Sol.Identities[sym].Verdict()` with `b`'s per route and raise `Flag("identity:"+sym, text)` on Broken, `Alarm` on Short, `Clear` on Holds.

**K14 — low — §5.6: "a stop clears itself once the identity holds at a height at or above T".** agent/signer.go:253-261 evaluates the identity at the own node's head when T is above it inside the allowance, and :328 `resume()` then clears any standing halt, the "client runs ahead" stop included — at a height below T, before the own node has reached the header the stop was about. Smallest fix: in `checkIdentity`, when `s.halted` is the ahead-stop, call `resume()` only if `uint64(head.Height) >= T`; otherwise keep the halt and note "the identity holds at the head, below T".

**K15 — low — §5.3: "`hlwatch` and `hlsol audit` print both counters and the gap".** cmd/hlsol/audit.go:270 prints the admin program's last sequence number and never reads r/hlgov (the audit takes `-mailbox-pkgpath` but no r/hlgov path, :376-396). **K16 — low — §13 kind 9: "the proposer and `hlwatch` check it before the vote, as they check kind 8's program".** cmd/hlwatch/main.go:296-305 lists a builder-made proposal by its description only; no RPC check of a kind 9's successor programdata (its upgrade authority must be none) or a kind 8's program (never the admin program) and buffer.
