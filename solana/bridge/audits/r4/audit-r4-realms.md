## Findings

### R27 — low — the predecessor's flags do not follow the lock: the successor is an unflagged route from its first page
Rule: PLAN §5.3 ("a function of a route is refused while any key's flag for it is set"; the meters restart only when an unpause or a removal clears a standing payouts flag), §5.9 ("nothing credited or paid under a suspect verifier"), R16's rule (a payment out of the lock waits under a payouts flag).
Files: realm/hlmailbox/flags.gno:301–311 (`AddRoute` registers a bare `routeRec`, copies no flag), 118–125 and 130–151 (`Paused` and `Flagged` keyed by the route's own path); realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1578–1583 (the migrate applies and registers the successor), 1685–1688 (page 0 moves the counters and the lock); realm/hlgnot/hlgnot.gno:454–468 (`Pull` pays the lock with no `mustNotBePaused`), 267–276 (`Claim` asks only the successor's own flags); hlbubble.gno:401–415, 252–261 the same.
Scenario: a migrate queued on day 0 applies on day 7. On day 6 the watch flagged `credits` and `payouts` on hlgnot over a forged flood and a guardian named the ids; the void waits its vote. The successor pulls page 0 and the claims: it is a route with no flag (the predecessor's stay keyed to the dead path), so the first `Claim` there pays the imported forged claims at the normal rate until a guardian re-flags the successor — a pause the plan says only an unpause or a removal lifts. The monitors' once-a-minute re-assertion closes it only if they already watch the new route.
Fix: in `AddRoute`, copy every standing flag of the calling route to the new path (seat, `at`, ids, range), so the pause follows the claims and clears there as before; and gate `Pull`'s page 0 with `mustNotBePaused(FnPayouts)` like a sweep (R16). Test: a flagged route migrated, the successor's `Claim` refused.

### R28 — low — a range void or a migrate drafted while pages are still arriving counts and closes over a partial record set
Rule: PLAN §5.3 (the 1,000-credit cap, "what it covers never grows after"; the migration's pause "from that page until the last"); M1-NOTES M1f's R23 and R20/AP4 readings.
Files: realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1004–1046 (`DraftVoid`'s range scan over `credits` as they stand, no `importing` test), 1769–1786 (`Import` adds claims at their old heights, inside any range closed earlier), 1249–1276 and 1451–1471 (the walks at execution cover them all), 1175–1184 (`DraftMigrate` has no `importing` test), 1755–1759 (`Import` refuses once `MigratedTo` is set).
Scenario: a successor is at page 3 of 40. GovDAO drafts `void as forged heights:H1-H2` over the 900 imported forged credits in the range, under the cap. Pages 4–40 bring 30,000 more credits at those heights (a flood is what a migration may be fleeing). At execution the at-once walk, or `apply`'s, visits 30,900 credits at ≈0.4M gas each — past the 3B block — so the proposal lapses on day 14, and GovDAO cannot split what the draft never saw. A migrate of the successor drafted mid-import applies a week on, and `MigratedTo` makes `Import` refuse the pages still at the first predecessor, which only the middle route may pull: the lock moved on, those claims stay owed on counters no record backs.
Fix: `DraftVoid`'s range form and `DraftMigrate` refuse while `s.importing` ("the predecessor's pages are still arriving"); `Decide`'s range branch and `apply`'s count the walk against `MaxRangeClaims` before voiding and panic above it. Test: a range drafted between two pages, the third page larger than the cap.

### R29 — low — kind 9 and the recipient swap accept a former recipient, a retired admin program
Rule: PLAN §13 kind 9 ("never the admin program's own id"), §5.3 ("a proposal that would fail on Solana is refused when it is made"); R8's rule, M1f part 2's.
Files: realm/hlgov/hlgov.gno:565–576 (`NewTransferToSuccessorRequest` refuses `p == recipient` only), 653–665 (`NewSetDecisionRecipientRequest` refuses the current recipient only), 684–700 (`Execute` overwrites `recipient` and keeps no history); bridge/programs/admin-program/src/processor.rs:149–157 (`successor_init` answers `NotInherited` for a program that never named a predecessor, or inherited already), 690–694 (a transfer that dies on the successor's init pends for good).
Scenario: A hands over to S by kind 9 and GovDAO swaps the recipient to S. A year on, a member proposes `transfer to successor A`: A is immutable, so hlwatch prints "immutable" and the vote passes; S executes it after 7 days, re-points every authority to A and dies on A's `successor_init` (`NotInherited`), so the decision pends until a cancel — a wasted vote and a standing line on hlwatch, the shape kind 8's and kind 9's self-refusals were added for. A recipient swap back to A sends every decision for 7 days to a program that answers `Retired`, re-dispatched in vain until swapped again.
Fix: keep `former [][32]byte` — every recipient `Execute` replaced — and refuse any of them in `NewTransferToSuccessorRequest`'s `check` and in `NewSetDecisionRecipientRequest`, at creation and in the closure. Test: a swap, then a kind 9 and a swap naming the old recipient.

### R30 — low — a refund has no lock floor: the realm's coin balance is its only guard
Rule: PLAN §5.3 (the sweep payment "never more than the lock holds", re-checked when it applies); R26's rule for a turn; §5.9 (the identity the watch reads).
Files: realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1559–1577 (`apply`'s refund branch: `s.Lock -= sd.Amount; env.Pay(…)` with no comparison to `Lock`), 1158–1168 (`DraftRefund` checks the send exists and is unrefunded only); contrast 1150–1152 and 1539–1541 (the sweep's bound, twice) and 629–631 (`Claim`'s cap); realm/hlgnot/hlgnot.gno:278–280 and hlbubble.gno:263 (the payment fails on an empty balance alone).
Scenario: a forgery paid 1,900 of a 2,000 lock before its void; GovDAO refunds alice's undelivered 500 send. On chain `SendCoins` fails 400 short and the decision stays pending behind a message about coins, retried by the relayers hourly until a cancel. Anyone who first sends 400 ugnot to the realm's address makes it succeed: `Lock` reads −400, `Cap()` falls to the floor, every turn skips (`avail ≤ 0`) until sends refill it, and the identity the watch reads is broken by 400 with the mistaken-void alarm.
Fix: in `apply`'s refund branch, `if sd.Amount > s.Lock { panic("hlroute: the lock holds less than the send's " + …) }` before the subtraction — the sum waits, as a sweep's does; `DraftRefund` sums its list against the lock at creation. Test: a refund above the lock.

## Checked and held

- Spec §5.1–§5.2, guide §5.6/§5.8, review 1 and 9: `AnnounceSuccessor`, `NewSweepRequest` and every closure M1f touched derive identity from their own live `cur.Previous()`; no secondary realm parameter in hl* or p/; the checker's one hit is realm/bubbletreasury, outside the bridge.
- Spec §5.5: `AnnounceSuccessor` refuses a sub-identity by `Subpath()` (pinned uverse.go:185) and any caller outside `gno.land/r/`; `DraftMigrate` and `AddRoute` refuse a `#` path; `Pull`'s successor and `mustBeRoute` are exact pkgpath matches, so an announcement allows and binds nothing — a vote still names the path, and nobody can announce a path they do not hold.
- Spec §5.3, guide §5.7, review 6: the announcement is a string in `successors`; no realm value in `sweepParams`, `Snapshot`, `rotateSeatsParams.held` or any M1f param.
- Spec §3.2 (copies type-driven), §4.1–§4.3, guide §2.1–§2.3, §5.1/§5.1a: `Env.Announced` is a closure declared in the route that only reads hlmailbox; `Export` copies claims by value under the predecessor and `Import` allocates the successor's own; `Pull`'s `*Snapshot` carries no mutator; `st` stays unexported.
- Spec §9, guide §6.1: every refusal that must not block a proposal is a skip with a count (list voids, restores, refunds, sweep entries, seat removals); what panics is meant to hold the decision for a retry or a cancel (a sum above `Sweep` or the lock, an unannounced successor, a hand credit above the lock).
- Review 10, spec §11.3: `successors/<from>` parses its offset and names at most 20; `Figures` counts by `nOpen`; `JSON` adds `importing`/`imported` and the mailbox's `successors`/`successorCount` after `routes`; every field the agents read keeps its name.
- The sweep queue by id (§5.3): a repeated id refused by `ParseIDs`, a voided entry only after its restore, the entries and their height index gone before the one payment, so a paid id is "done already" to `DraftCredit` and nothing to a void, a range or a second payment; the counters never go negative (`Sweep` floors in `voidSweep` and `creditByVote`, unreachable through the builders and tested white-box); a sum the lock no longer covers waits.
- The migration (§5.3): pages in order each once on both sides, a re-pulled page empty and refused by `Import`'s order check, the counters and the lock once with page 0, `QueueNext` final on the predecessor (nothing credits there after the migrate applied) and the successor's counter raised at first sight, so a hand credit, a restore or a credit by vote between pages lands above every imported place; `importing` from page 0 to the page with `More == false`, refusing `Credit` alone — `Claim`, `Execute`, the flags and the mailbox stay open, and a payout there is backed since the lock arrived with page 0; a burn in flight to the predecessor stays undelivered (`mustNotBeMigrated` before anything) and is the successor's hand credit, "done already" in the shared mailbox for any id a later page carries; a page's loss is impossible since `Pull` and `Import` run in the successor's one transaction.
- Rotate seats (§5.3 v31): `held` at creation; at-once removals vacate only an unchanged holder, spend when none was left, and say which seats were skipped; the queued path still refuses a changed seat set whole (`before`) and skips a changed holder; the void builder's and the unpause's removals the same (R17).
- Ranges: the cap counts every credit visited; a range closes at the creation height and `eachInRange`'s upper key (`heightKey(To+1, "")`) never overflows since `To` is at most that height; `since:` covers the verifier's credits only; a start past its end visits nothing (avl `TraverseInRange`); the note's "last voided at height" names an at-once or a delayed walk's last height, and a next range starting after it re-covers only what R22 skips.
- `Claim` (§5.4): a turn is min(bridge meter, quarter, lock), the fee whole first, a dry lock or meter a skip with the cursor advanced, at most 4n visits.
- r/hlgov (§13): bodies under 600 (above); kinds 10 and 11 absent; kinds 8 and 9 refuse the recipient the realm knows at both clocks; `Redispatch` re-sends a body that passed; `mustRaise` bounded by 2^32.
- Clocks and the DAO frame: `made` at the builder call, the deadline the only error, `mustBeDAO` and `mustBeLive` in every M1f closure, `Execute`'s payouts gate for a sweep and a refund (R16); hlfees, hlverifier, decisions and hyperlane unchanged since round 3.
- A mis-routed hand credit (hlgnot's `MarkDone` of a BUBBLE burn's id) has no undo, and the burn is "done already" on its own route; the cure is a credit under a fresh id there (the watch skips vote credits) — a member's error with a workaround, not a finding. An announcement proves a realm at the path, not that it is public: §8.3's member check must read the successor's gnomod for `private`.

## Verdict

Rounds 1–3's fixes hold, R19–R26 and both part-2 guards included; the realms meet the specification and the guide on every function M1f added or changed. Nothing critical, high or medium. Four lows, each a guard of a line or two: the predecessor's flags do not follow the lock to its successor (R27), a range void or a migrate drafted while pages still arrive sees a partial record set (R28), kind 9 and the recipient swap accept a retired predecessor (R29), and a refund has no lock floor (R30). The round converges on the realms.
