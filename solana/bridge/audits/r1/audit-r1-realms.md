# Audit 4, round 1 — the gno.land realms as built (tree be69e1c, 2026-10-13)

Read: PLAN.md §5.1–5.5, §5.9, §13; M1-NOTES.md; every file under realm/hlmailbox, hlverifier, hlgnot, hlbubble, hlfees, hlgov, p/decisions/v0, p/hlroute/v0, p/hyperlane/v0, tests included; realm/bubble's token API and p/grc20votes' TransferFrom; gno v1.2.0 as pinned (`~/go/pkg/mod/github.com/gnolang/gno@v1.2.0`): r/gov/dao's proxy, types and impl/v0 (the 4-arg `NewSimpleExecutor`, `Execute` gated on the proxy's frame, `PreExecuteProposal` setting Accepted before the callback, an error → Denied only under `ExecuteOrRejectProposal`), chain/runtime (`IsUserCall`, `AssertOriginCall` = first frame a MsgCall and ≤2 call frames), chain/runtime/unsafe (`OriginSend` marks the envelope observed only for the paid realm), chain/banker (an OriginSend banker spends only from `OriginSendRecipient`), the keeper's unobserved-envelope refusal, `revive` test-only (`ReviveEnabled`), p/nt/avl's range semantics, and the Go relayer's `Execute` loop.

## Findings

### R1 — medium — a queued signer rotation re-adds a key removed at once, applied automatically
realm/hlmailbox/gov.gno:206–216 (the at-once test), 440–442 (`Execute` applies `p.keys, p.rule` as named); bridge/agent/relayer.go:505–540 (the relayers call `Execute` on every due decision); the test pins the behaviour at realm/hlmailbox/mailbox_test.gno:388–393 (decision 4 applied after decision 5).
Scenario: GovDAO queues "A,B,C,D under 2" (an addition, 7 days). On day 3 signer B is found compromised and a pure removal "A,C,D under 2" executes at once. On day 7 the relayer's loop calls `Execute(id)` on the queued addition, which sets the set to A,B,C,D: B is back, with no vote and no alarm, unless GovDAO noticed and cancelled within four days. `Execute` re-checks nothing about the signer state the proposal was decided against, and queued decisions never expire.
Fix: when a rotation is queued, store the signer set and rule it was decided against (`signerKeys`, `signerRule` at execution of the proposal) in its params; `Execute` panics unless they still equal the live ones, so any intervening change forces a fresh proposal. Same guard for queued `rotate seats`.

### R2 — medium — under the two-group rule no signer removal is ever at once
realm/hlmailbox/gov.gno:165–192 (`parseSigners`: every group member must be a signer), 199–217 (`rule.equal(signerRule)` is part of the at-once test); realm/hlverifier/hlverifier.gno:76–86.
Scenario: the rule is "one of A={J} and one of B={K,L}" (§11.13). K is compromised. A proposal naming keys J,L with B={L} changes the rule → queued 7 days; one naming B={K,L} unchanged is refused at creation ("every group member must be a signer"). So the plan's instant cut ("a 2-of-3 rule is still met by the 2 that remain after one removal; one of each group under §11.13's rule, so removing the partner is never at once") is unreachable for any group with two or more members: K keeps co-signing for a week.
Fix: in the closure, derive `pruned` = the live rule with every key absent from `ks` dropped from both groups; take the at-once path when `rule.equal(pruned) && subset(ks, signerKeys) && len(ks) < len(signerKeys) && satisfiable(ks)`, and set `signerKeys, signerRule = ks, rule` there.

### R3 — low — the void's unpause option vacates any number of seats at once, the two-guardian cap bypassed
realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:700–719 (`ParseSeats`: up to six, no guardian count), 732–762, 906–908 (`env.RemoveSeat` per seat); realm/hlgnot/hlgnot.gno:369–378 and realm/hlbubble/hlbubble.gno:327–336 (only "seat non-empty" is checked); contrast realm/hlmailbox/gov.gno:270–272 (`rotate seats`: two guardians at most) and 351–364 (`unpause`: one seat).
Scenario: one void proposal with `removeSeats "1,2,3,4,5,6"` vacates all four guardians and both watch keys in the executing transaction, their flags with them (meters restarted), leaving nobody able to pause until a 7-day replacement lands. §5.3 caps guardian changes at two per proposal; the cap lives in one builder and not in the other two paths to the same effect.
Fix: in `DraftVoid` (and `NewUnpauseRequest`'s `removeSeat`), count guardian seats among `RemoveSeats` and refuse more than two, the same rule as `parseSeatChanges`.

### R4 — low — kind 6 enrolls any address as a router for gno.land's own domain
realm/hlgov/hlgov.gno:467–475.
Scenario: a proposal "enroll GNOT's router for domain 1198419788: <attacker's address as bytes32>" passes (members read a 64-hex string). Seven days later the synthetic GNOT program accepts every `Dispatch` from that address as a mint order: `hlmailbox.Dispatch` is open to anyone, so unbacked synthetic GNOT is minted without a single lock. The only legitimate routers for the local domain are registered routes (`hlmailbox.IsRoute`, successors included via `AddRoute`); the builder, which "validates parameters with the admin program's own rules", can know that.
Fix: in `NewEnrollRouterRequest`, when `domain == hlmailbox.LocalDomain()` require `router` to equal `AddressToBytes32(chain.PackageAddress(path))` for some path in `hlmailbox.Routes()`; re-check in the closure.

### R5 — low — a recipient's meter starts up to a quarter full after the first payouts epoch
realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:93–96 (the new-epoch branch sets `At = epochAt`), 325–334 (`queue`: a fresh queue has `Epoch 0`, `At = now`).
Scenario: an unpause bumped the route's epoch to 1 ten days ago. A first-ever burn to a fresh recipient is credited; at its first `Claim` turn `m.Epoch (0) != epoch (1)` resets the meter "as of epochAt", ten days back, and the refill (capped at a day) fills the quarter at once. M1-NOTES: "a recipient's meter starts empty when its queue is made"; §5.4: both meters start empty. The bridge meter still bounds the total, so this moves money between recipients rather than past the cap.
Fix: in `queue`, initialise the meter with the current epoch: give `queue` the `epoch` argument (callers have it) and set `q.meter.Epoch = epoch`, so the reset branch is never taken for a queue made after the bump.

### R6 — low — the routes' pages render every waiting claim and every sweep entry
realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:1197–1217; realm/hlgnot/hlgnot.gno:470–485; realm/hlbubble/hlbubble.gno:412–427.
Scenario: a burn of one base unit on Solana costs its sender a Solana fee and lands here as a sweep entry (amount below the fee), the relayer paying the gno gas and deposit. Ten thousand of them make `Render("")` — the page §8.3 tells a member to read, and the one the launch notice links — exceed the query gas, and the page fails until GovDAO sweeps (one decision per address, by amount, not by id, so the entries never leave). `json` and `claim/<id>` stay bounded.
Fix: cap both lists at a page (say 100 lines, oldest first, with a count of the rest) and add `claims/<from>` and `sweeps/<from>` paths, as hlmailbox's `msgs/<from>` does.

### R7 — low — a credited amount larger than the lock is accepted; the counters can wrap
realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:356–373, 375–386 (`s.Owed += amount` with no bound but `maxI64`); realm/hlgnot/hlgnot.gno:238–240.
Scenario: the Solana supply is never above the lock (a mint needs a lock, a burn needs supply), so a burn above `s.Lock` cannot be honest; the realm still writes it as a claim, and its quarter starts paying out under the meters until a guardian names it. Two such claims near 2^62 base units wrap `Owed` (int64, no overflow check in gno), after which the page, the identity the signers and the watch compute, and `void`'s arithmetic read garbage.
Fix: in `Credit`, route `amount > s.Lock` to the sweep queue like a realm-address burn (owed, paying nobody until GovDAO directs it), which also bounds every claim by the lock; keep the `maxI64` check.

### R8 — low — kind 8 does not refuse the admin program's own id
realm/hlgov/hlgov.gno:492–499 (checks non-zero and `program != buffer` only); §13: "8 `upgrade` (program 32, never the admin program's own id; …)".
Scenario: a proposal "upgrade <admin program> from buffer B" is accepted on gno.land and dispatched; the admin program's authority is burned, so the decision is spent on Solana after its 7 days — a wasted vote and a false line on `hlwatch`'s pending list, where §13 says the builder refuses it.
Fix: add `p == recipient` to the refusal in `NewUpgradeRequest`, as `NewTransferToSuccessorRequest` does.

### R9 — low — the deadline "denied for good" exists only under `ExecuteOrRejectProposal`
realm/hlmailbox/gov.gno:69–77 (the closure returns the deadline error), realm/p/decisions/v0/decisions.gno:146–153; gno v1.2.0 r/gov/dao/proxy.gno `executeProposal`: under `ExecuteProposal` an executor error panics and the transaction, the Denied mark included, reverts; bridge/local/e2e.sh:235,377,405,439 call `ExecuteProposal`.
Scenario: a passed Launch (or any builder's proposal) is executed on day 15 with `ExecuteProposal`, the member's natural call: the error aborts the transaction, nothing is recorded, the proposal stays "has the votes and no execution" on `hlwatch` forever, and every retry fails the same way. Harmless to the realms (the decision is never recorded) but the plan's and the page's words ("denied for good") are false for that path.
Fix: the member tooling and the runbook execute builder-made proposals with `ExecuteOrRejectProposal`; the mailbox page says so beside "lapses and is proposed again".

### R10 — low — a fee above a recipient's quarter stalls every first installment
realm/p/hlroute/v0/hlroute.gno:640–652 (`DraftFee` bounds the fee below only), 470–478 (`avail < fee → continue`).
Scenario: GovDAO raises the fee past `CapFloor/4` (a typo of one zero after a floor lowering). Every new claim's first turn needs the whole fee from the recipient's quarter-meter, which can never hold it: payouts stop for every new recipient, with no flag and no alarm, until a lowering lands.
Fix: `DraftFee` and `apply(feeParams)` refuse `fee > s.CapFloor/4`; `DraftCapFloor` refuses `n/4 < s.Fee`.

## Checked and held

- Authorization: every mutator takes a live `cur`; `MarkDone`/`ClearFlags`/`VacateSeat`/`AddRoute` are route-only by pkgpath, `SetFlag`/`ClearFlag` seat-only by address, `Withdraw` treasurer-only, `Pull` successor-only; builders have no side effect at creation; closures run only from r/gov/dao's frame (v1.2.0's `SimpleExecutor.Execute` gates on the proxy, so `cur.Previous()` is the DAO) and every closure re-checks `live`, the deadline and its own preconditions.
- `Process`: done mark before `Verify`, a `Verify` error panics and `revive` is test-only on chain, so the mark reverts with the transaction; id = keccak of the whole message, destination/version/recipient checked in the mailbox, origin and router (old one 7 days) in the route; `Dispatch` and `Reemit` emit §13's seven attributes from hlmailbox's own frame, with `hash` = sha256 of the bytes.
- Signers: the threshold rule's at-once removal needs the rule unchanged, a strict subset and the active verifier's `Satisfiable` in the executing transaction; every signature in metadata must verify, in increasing index order; a zero-threshold rule fails closed; the digest binds origin, hook, root, index and id, so a test-chain signature cannot replay on mainnet.
- Seats and flags: keyed by seat, cleared on vacate/replace on every route, id lists ≤256 and guardians-only, watch keys name nothing, `Flagged` counts guardians only, the epoch bumps only when a standing payouts flag is cleared by an unpause, a removal or the void option — never by a key's own clear.
- Clocks: 7 and 14 days by block time; `made` is the builder call's block time; a lowering/cancel/withheld-restore/flagged void at once, the rest queued; a failed unpause is spent, never queued; `Launch` is one-shot (`live` only set there), carries the deadline, and every other builder but the cancels refuses before it.
- Meters: the refill carries its remainder exactly (checked by hand and by the tests), never above the ceiling, the cap re-read per turn so it only falls with the lock; quarter per recipient; the fee is the first installment, whole; turns by cursor with ≤4n visits; the per-message maximum and minimum at send only; the cap floor never below the launch floor, the maximum never below the minimum.
- The identity: every transition of send, credit, sweep, claim, void (open/withheld/done → withheld/forged), restore (both), credit-by-vote (when GovDAO names the remainder, as M1 says), sweep payment, refund, export and import keeps `lock = sent − paid` and moves `supply + owed + voided − lost` only where the plan says it moves; no path was found that breaks it silently.
- GNOT envelope: exact `{amount+fee ugnot}` read with `unsafe.OriginSend` (which marks it observed, so the keeper accepts the call), `IsUserCall` then `AssertOriginCall`, the fee spent with an OriginSend banker from the paid realm, the rest left as the lock; refunds and payouts with fresh RealmSend bankers; BUBBLE through the caller's allowance to the route, with a balance-delta check; realm-address recipients refused at send and swept on the way in; no reentrancy surface (coin sends and `ledger.move` run no code).
- r/hlgov: every body matches §13 byte for byte (kind 1 `salt ‖ form ‖ …`, kinds 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9); kind 4 refused; two cancels of one list differ by the decision number inside the salt; `sync sequence` raise-only at creation and execution; the compiled start; re-dispatch keeps the body; no free text reaches a description (every raw string is parsed to digits, keys or addresses).
- hlfees: `Withdraw` to the treasurer alone, everything held; the treasurer swap 7 days, cancellable.
- Storage and gas: `sent`, `done`, `sends`, `claims` grow only at a payer's cost; `Paused`, `Flagged`, `Flags`, `clearSeat`, the mailbox pages (10/20 lines) and the decision pages are bounded; `oldestOpen` walks only a recipient's own voided claims.
- Tests' blind spots: no fresh-recipient meter after an epoch bump (R5); no two-group removal (R2); no stale queued rotation after an at-once removal (pinned as correct, R1); no void with more than two guardian seats (R3); no amount above the lock (R7); GovDAO's retry-after-panic cannot be exercised (the harness keeps Accepted; the e2e only ever uses `ExecuteProposal`, R9); hlbubble's pause, void and migrate paths are covered only through hlgnot.

## Verdict

Nothing critical or high. The money paths — the envelope, the allowance, the meters, the claims' queues, the void/restore/credit state machine and the five counters — hold on the gno side as built, and the realm boundaries are drawn the way v1.2.0 enforces them. Two mediums are governance-clock gaps the plan's safety story depends on: a queued rotation undoing an at-once removal (R1, automatic through the relayers), and no at-once removal under the two-group rule (R2). The eight lows are guards worth a line; R4 and R7 close the two cheapest ways to mint or book what no lock backs.
