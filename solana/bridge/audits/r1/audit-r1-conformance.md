## 3. Findings (the rules implemented differently or not at all)

**K1 — medium — §6.1, §6.4: the validator-set history is bounded at 4 and can lose the settled set.** Plan: "the validator-set history (one entry per set change …)", and "a hold begun before a rotation keeps the signers at the pre-hold set for as long as it stands". Code: `HISTORY_MAX = 4` (bridge/programs/gno-light-client-ism/src/config.rs:31) and `push_history` evicts index 0 (state.rs:95-100); `settled_set` (admin-program/src/recovery.rs:75-80) finds nothing once the pre-hold entry is gone and every later entry's quarantine end is at or after the hold's start, so `recovery_init`/`recovery_record` answer `NotSettled`. Scenario: a hold stands; gno.land's governance makes four validator-set changes (two additions, each an add at power 1 then a raise — the chain's own pattern); the recovery window opens on the hold's 14th day and nobody can sign anything until the hold ends. Smallest fix: never evict the newest entry whose quarantine has ended (keep the settled anchor), evicting the next-oldest instead, and raise `HISTORY_MAX` (the body region has 2 KB of slack and `Grow`).

**K2 — medium — §13: "`Bytes32ToAddress` requires the top 12 bytes to be zero" is not implemented.** realm/p/hyperlane/v0/hyperlane.gno:106-112 encodes `b[12:]` unchecked; `Process` credits the result (hlgnot.gno:245, hlbubble.gno:229); bridge/hl/hl.go's `Bytes32ToGnoAddress` truncates the same way, so the monitor's diverted-credit check (agent/monitor.go:633) compares two identical truncations and sees nothing. Scenario: a Solana user burns to a raw Solana pubkey; the claim is credited to the address of its last twenty bytes, which nobody holds; the cure is two votes (void as withheld, credit by id). Smallest fix: in both routes' `Process` refuse a recipient whose first twelve bytes are not zero (the message then stays undelivered and GovDAO credits it by id to its owner), and make the Go reader return an error so the monitor flags such a credit.

**K3 — medium — §6.3, §6.4, §5.9: no tool drives a recovery.** Plan: "the published tool … drives a devnet recovery drill"; the assent "needs … a signing tool for their consensus keys (§11.10), a gate of the launch vote"; §5.9's drills "by both triggers". Code: the builders exist (bridge/sol/admin.go:212-251) and the vectors pin them; neither `hlsol` nor `hlbridge` stages, collects signatures over the digest, records, or cancels under the standing anchor (M3c "Gaps"). Smallest fix: an `hlsol recover` command (init, verify from detached ed25519 signatures over the 32-byte digest, record, close; a cancel under the anchor `hlwatch` prints) before the drills.

**K4 — low — §5.9: the realms carry no clock factor.** Plan: "the test chain's realms carry the same factor on their 7-day delay (a compiled constant beside §5.3's launch values …), and the factor divides … every delay of the realms, the old router's 7 days included". Code: p/decisions `Delay = 7 * Day`, `Deadline = 14 * Day` (decisions.gno:21-24), hlroute's `OldUntil = now + Delay` (hlroute.gno:1006); no factor anywhere. The 72-hour runs cannot exercise a gno.land 7-day delay. Smallest fix: a compiled `Factor` in p/decisions (1 at launch) dividing `Delay`, `Deadline` and the old-router window, printed on the page.

**K5 — low — §13 kind 8 / §6.5 "the builders apply the same checks up front"; §13 kind 9 "the builder checks the same by RPC".** Code: `NewUpgradeRequest` (hlgov.gno:492-499) accepts the admin program's own id as the program (the admin program spends it a week later); `NewTransferToSuccessorRequest` (hlgov.gno:504-510) cannot read Solana. Smallest fix: refuse `p == recipient` in kind 8's builder; reword kind 9's sentence to "a member checks by RPC before proposing" (M1's reading).

**K6 — low — §5.7 "no single transaction's error pins the cursor".** Code: `solScan` (agent/chain.go:456-472) returns the cursor before a transaction it cannot read, so one persistently unreadable transaction stops the signers' and the relayer's scans (M3a reads the clause as "no delivery error"). Scenario: provider 1 cannot serve one transaction; signing of every later burn waits on a human. Smallest fix: the plan says "no delivery error pins the cursor; an unreadable transaction does, by design, and the status says which" — or the scan falls back to provider 2 for that transaction.

**K7 — low — §5.5 "amount-0 transfers … skipped by the relayer".** Code: `solToGno` (agent/relayer.go:292-329) carries any amount; the realm sweeps a 0 (hlroute.gno:365-370), writing a sweep record and a done mark for nothing. Smallest fix: skip `amount == 0` in `solToGno` (`token()` already parses it) and refuse it in `Credit`.

**K8 — low — §5.3 credit by id "so the identity does not move".** Code: `creditByVote` (hlroute.gno:1057-1074) moves owed by (amount − old remainder). The clause is true only when GovDAO names the remainder. Smallest fix: the plan says "replaces the restored remainder with the recipient GovDAO names (and the amount, which moves owed by the difference)", or the builder refuses an amount other than the remainder for a voided claim.

**K9 — low — §6.1 "no decision shortens [the quarantine]".** Code: kind 11's reset clears it (client processor.rs:676-677) and sets the last-accepted time (M2 choice 5). Smallest fix: the plan names the exception ("no decision but a recovery's reset, whose set is settled by definition").

**K10 — low — §13 fixed seeds: a staged proof is "(`proof`, submitter, proof hash)".** Code: (`proof`, submitter, sha256 of the message) (client processor.rs:566-573; sol/client.go:51-53), and the recovery stage (`recovery`, submitter, digest) is missing from the list. Smallest fix: the plan's list says "message hash" and adds the recovery stage's seed.

**K11 — low — §6.5 the successor "takes its four seats and watch slots as they stand at the transfer".** Code: the successor's seats, `last_seq` and `recovery_counter` are whatever its own `init` was given (admin-program/src/processor.rs:89-113); kind 9's execution (processor.rs:698-753) checks only that the successor is immutable. Scenario: a successor initialised with other seats inherits every authority after one 7-day vote. Smallest fix: kind 9's Execute reads the successor's `state` PDA and spends the decision unless its seats, counters and `setup_done` match the predecessor's, or the plan says "given as its init data, which §8.3's audit checks against the predecessor's before the vote".

**K12 — low — §5.8 `hlwatch` shows "every recovery against the validators' signatures".** Code: views_sol.go:369-402 lists pending recovery decisions and the window; no recorded recovery is checked against who signed. Smallest fix: fold `recovery_verify` transactions' precompile entries into hlwatch's record (as the header record is) and print, per recorded recovery, the signers and their power.

## 4. Verdict

The code follows §5, §6 and §13 closely: 190 rules enumerated, 170 as written, 14 differently (mostly readings the notes declare), 6 not implemented (gaps the notes own). Two medium findings change what the plan promises — the client's four-entry set history can evict the settled set under a hold (K1), and `Bytes32ToAddress`'s zero-top check is absent on the credit path (K2) — and one gate of §5.9 has no code yet (the recovery driver, K3). The nine low findings are wording the plan should adopt from the notes or one-line refusals. Nothing above medium.
